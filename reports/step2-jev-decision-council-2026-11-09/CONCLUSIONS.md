# Council: whether to keep Jev in clean Step 2

2026-11-09. Alex asked whether Jev is still the way to work. Codex and Claude independently reviewed the completed 54-call same-input diagnostic, the stored actual candidate decisions, original sources, historic frozen decisions and other prior experiments. They challenged the first conclusions and converged. Handoffs, original outputs and challenge responses are preserved in this directory; Claude's first challenge hit a usage limit and was completed on an available Claude model, with the final exchange separately recorded.

**Decision recommendation:** retain Jev as the current *clean Step 2 working baseline* while investigating its failures. This is the existing implemented and exercised full-candidate workflow under D381, and the proposed alternative was only evaluated on single pairs and has no demonstrated group ranking. That is an implementation and evidence-state reason to keep it for now, not proof Jev is the most accurate choice, that it is required forever, that it is fixed, or that the clean path is production-ready. The independent comparator must not be adopted from this study.

**Evidence and limitations:**
- On the pre-selected H01 pair, full Jev missed the repeated acquisition announcement 3/3 and Jev single missed 3/3, while DeepSeek single identified the repeat 3/3. These are repeated requests on one case; provider-side draws are not guaranteed independent. On B05, DeepSeek misidentified the company 70% forecast as a revision of customer-indicated potential in 3/3; Jev single classified different 3/3, while full Jev classified different 1/3. This shows the observed answers differed with request form, but does not establish that other candidates caused the difference or prove Jev is globally more accurate. The two providers, model and output contract also differ.
- Fresh c8 evidence: 1 clearly wrong merge in 57 plus two overlaps, one drawn 50-article sample; earlier a different run had 3 wrong of 104, which failed the 2% line. These use different upstream inputs and are not a controlled version comparison or a time trend. Neither denominator measures missed repeats or the accuracy of revision links. The search result 141/143 is conditional on known saved counterparts and one 11-member story contributes 55 pair comparisons; revisions hardly appear. It is not total recall. Detail retention 22/26 vs 22/26 is a storage check, not proof of reader interpretation.
- R05 (Shield) remains a genuine source/contract dispute. P01 confirms that the official-denial target can be recognized, but the earlier article already includes the denial, and an extra edge from the complete Jev request remains disputed. Do not treat one target as proof the complete outcome or all revision behavior is right.
- The `embedding scores` trial was interrupted after six cases and did not reach the key error, so its lack of visible benefit is inconclusive. A .5 decision threshold blocked one reader-marked wrong merge but also four accepted and one overlapping decisions in fixed historical replays; repeated articles mean those 363 are not independent. The stricter “nothing new” criterion reduced 102 merges to 22, including legitimate repeats. Neither is established as a fix.
- A historical old-contract 2026-10-27 exploration compared Jev with a different generative analysis that saw extensive context and Jev hints. A post-answer gold change made an apparent 20/21 action result invalid; before that change its action score was 10/21 plus 2/3 held out. Its inputs, tasks and historical definitions differ from clean Step 2. It shows alternatives are worth studying but does not establish an equal-input ranking.
- Full current code sends every supersedes label as an edge and retains the individual texts; wrong edges and grouping can mislead readers, but the direct effect on an investment judgment has not been measured. An isolated library is not a finished production writer.

**Recommended next check, not yet performed:** trace the 55 subsequent members labeled repeat in the pre-run source-backed reference `poc/data/clean-acceptance-2026-11-09/settled.json`, across 26 groups. Verify matter-to-record mapping and bundled claims before calculating any result. Separately count source-not-extracted, mapping ambiguous, search candidate missing, and search found but not ultimately grouped; inspect every alleged miss against the original articles and sample successful mappings. H01 is a positive control: it must show search found the earlier candidate, Jev chose different and the record was placed in a new group. Preserve the frozen reference; do not silently relabel it. One story has 11 members and contributes 55 of 145 pair comparisons; these 55 members are a different unit, and are not 55 independent facts. Only 1 source reference member is marked revision. This analysis informs the repeat-miss issue, not global reliability, end-to-end impact or whether Jev outperforms DeepSeek. A later, separate review of the 56 D392 d1 `supersedes` answers is still required for revision behavior. No new threshold or acceptance line is adopted.
