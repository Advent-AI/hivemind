# Independent position before council

Objective: decide whether Jev remains the sensible working approach for clean Step 2 given its product contract, historical evidence and the new six-pair test, not whether it is perfect or should be replaced as a general truth.

Provisional position: continue Jev as the current comparison baseline, without presenting it as validated or fixed. The comparator experiment is deliberately selected and reveals one clear B05 regression versus fresh Jev single and one clear H01 improvement; neither model nor an ensemble is proven overall better. Provider, format and candidate batch differ, and pair labels do not select a group. R05 is contested. A three-draw single 6-case comparison cannot establish overall precision/recall. Jev remains a three-way judge over search results; retrieval recall, the validity of revisions, single-to-batch sensitivity, false group splits and correct persistence all matter. Actual Jev output `supersedes` does not itself make text disappear; source records remain stored, though relationships could mislead downstream readers. Baseline numerical lines: D381 fresh search 141/145 full-system available reference pairs, 1 clear wrong merge /57 plus 2 overlaps, detail retention 22=22; one draw, narrow reference, clustered 55 pairs and almost no revisions. None is overall precision or recall.

Challenge needed: whether the 56 supersedes conditional population is sufficiently source-verified to warrant a decision; whether a high score threshold or recheck layer could rescue Jev without false splits; whether missing fields or batching explain errors; whether implementing any fix is justified yet; and whether Jev-only is an endorsed production-ready architecture or simply the best available frozen reference. Compare against prior experiments and exact decisions, show false benefits and limits. No new paid run, production changes or reference revisions.
