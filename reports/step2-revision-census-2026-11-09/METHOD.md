# Revision-answer source census

2026-11-09: Alex authorized source review of all 56 saved D392 clean-d1 Step 2 candidate answers labeled supersedes, plus two blinded source readers and true revision control. The population is all such answers, not 56 independent incoming articles. `build_packet.py` reads the completed run and its completed predecessor in SQLite read-only mode. It resolves exact incoming IDs, actual compared original group records, complete source IDs, actual outputs and all group links. Preconditions: 56 answers, each linked in the saved outcome; 34 distinct incoming records; record IDs unique; source text is identical if present in both segments; all source IDs found; earlier original record precedes incoming article; known R05 pair 203:e1 → 119:e1 is present. Every source quotation (236 passages in all 76 pairs) is present in its saved complete article under whitespace normalization. Source article order is not proof of the time of the original claim or response.

There are 37 answers accompanying repeat placement and 19 with revision outcomes; all 56 appear in their per-record outcomes. The saved library consolidates repeated links between the same two groups: 30 retain the particular incoming record as their `by` field and 26 correspond to an already-present group link attributed to another incoming record. Every one of the 56 has its destination group represented, but they are not 56 separately stored edges. 20 sampled other-choice candidate comparisons are mixed and shuffled as controls, so the blind readers cannot infer any specific original Jev answer from case inclusion. Controls are not representative and have not been source-adjudicated in advance. The key identifies candidate outcomes and is not shown to the readers. A fixed seed 20261109 determines the sample and shuffle, and six packet SHA-256 hashes are printed by the builder. Source readers independently receive the same 76 source-only pairs, the present PRD definition, and which record fields Jev did or did not see, without receiving historic choices or other reader answers. The saved record is compared with the saved group original, not an arbitrary later member.

Each reader is asked to identify the stored and incoming main claim, exact source evidence, timing, source attribution, comparison relation and missing information, and to preserve uncertainty. Their outputs are source assessments, not a replacement frozen reference, and agreement is not adjudicated by majority vote. Original articles report claims; source review does not verify those external claims as fact. Important limitations: same news events recur, many relations share old or new records, the controls are deliberately sampled, and this one chosen run cannot establish population-wide Jev precision/recall or investor impact.

The separate `check_archived_requests.py` reconstructed the whole comparison question set and incoming state for every one of the 76 selected pairs against the read-only saved Jev archive. All 76 matched exactly one archived request; each matched candidate's original choice and all three probabilities equal the decision record. This confirms which text was actually presented for these pairs; it does not validate the contents or the Jev choice. The checker does not send requests or alter the archive.
