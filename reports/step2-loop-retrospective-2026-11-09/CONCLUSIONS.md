# Retrospective: stopping the Jev analysis loop

2026-11-09. Alex asked why the Jev work was going in circles and requested a Codex–Claude council. Codex independently read the source runs, prior decisions, actual compare code and previous council conclusions (`codex-reflection.md`); an independent Codex reader challenged the reflection and exact candidate wording (`independent-challenge.md`, `draft-review.md`); Claude Code read the same primary materials and its first view (`claude-r1.md`) was challenged in a resumed conversation, producing `claude-r2.md`. The exact handoffs, session ID and streams are kept here. This was a read-only retrospective of product data; this report and the review artifacts are new, but no production prompt, configuration, frozen gold or saved run was changed, and no Jev API was sent.

## What went wrong

The repeated question was whether Jev can err on the identity or scope of a revision. The 54-call six-pair test, the 55-member repeat investigation and the 56-answer revision census already answered that specific question with source-backed examples, ambiguity and counterexamples. After requiring the 56-answer census before deciding, Codex concluded by recommending that the same cases be classified again by input visibility, even though the two independent source readers already captured input limitations and the report itself checked both cases with and without explicit target information. The further work was not tied to a decision it would change. That was analysis without a defined stopping point, not a meaningful next experiment.

Codex also treated Jev's lack of natural-language reasoning as a reason not to move forward. The limitation is real: this typed choice interface does not reveal the model's reasoning. It does not justify an indefinite promise of further traces. A proposed change must be tested and audited, and the lack of a causal account must remain disclosed. A choice to keep Jev as the working baseline was already made on the available evidence; reopening that architectural question each cycle was unproductive. We cannot say the impact on the final investment judgment has been measured.

## Findings already sufficient to frame one test

In the selected completed run, 56 candidate answers attached revision labels to 34 incoming records; these lead to 30 distinct saved group relations. After two independent AI source readings and source-based challenges, 9 of the 30 had different main claims, 5 were repeat reports, 5 involved a directly addressed change/response, and 11 were uncertain. These counts are conditional on this run, depend on source-reader interpretation, repeat stories and do not estimate general precision. C036's actual model input states that the NVIDIA response concerns a Kyber NVL144 delay while the old main claim concerns a backup rack; Jev still chose revision. C057 compares the terms of a financing arrangement to the identity of a participant. The problem cannot simply be assumed to be missing context, although C007 demonstrates some context really is missing from the model input. Source readers disagree about R05/C059 and other cases; do not turn a majority into gold.

The recommendation is **one experimental intervention**, not another census: examine a Jev question that first identifies the statement the incoming text actually responds to, then compares it to the old record's main statement. This changes the procedure the model is asked to follow, without a specific-company rule. The current Jev criterion already says the same topic is insufficient; therefore the proposal could be just a paraphrase in effect, and cannot be called a root fix or improvement without a controlled result. Its first drafts and independent review are in `draft-candidate.md` and `draft-review.md`; they remain unsuitable to send. The prompt must preserve direct and implicitly supported responses, official confirmation as well as denial under D168, uncertainty about source timing, and the existing selection of one best repeat group; it must not mandate an explicit exact name or assume that a refusal to confirm/deny always resolves the relationship. The full request must be audited against PRD §5 and the decisions; prompt growth must be explained/cut as required by AGENTS §6. No score cutoff or second model has earned adoption.

The smallest proposed check is a same-day original-versus-candidate comparison on a small set of archived full candidate requests: include C036 and C057 as differing-statement leads; C035 as a specific NVL576 response; C073 as a source-reported policy change, not proof of a completed purchase; C021 as an official-denial target control with the known issue that the prior source already carried the denial; a previously valid repeat such as B01; and separately report contested C059. Preserve every candidate's answer, selected repeat group and all revision relations, and use the original version's own variability as a control. Stop the line if it loses supported updates/repeats or fails to improve decisive examples beyond control variability. A small result can reject the idea or justify a later held-out test; it cannot prove production improvement, an overall precision/recall or downstream benefit. The request reconstruction, isolated new sender, audit, exact spending reservation and preflight are NOT completed for this new candidate, and the test was NOT sent.

## Corrections from the council challenge

Claude initially suggested deleting official confirmation because it appeared only in old wording. That conflicts with D168, which expressly includes official confirmation or denial; Claude retracted it. Its suggestion that revision quality was out of scope because it is not a D381 acceptance line was wrong: Alex explicitly asked to strengthen Jev and examine these cases; Claude retracted it. Claude also withdrew a plan for roughly 240 calls, an unsupported cost estimate, a literal explicit-target requirement, and a claim that the latest request builder had verified actual transport bytes. The builder checked parsed request equality, and the prior six-case sender cannot be assumed to handle new cases without review. It also withdrew treating the R05 dispute as definitively resolved.

The initial candidate's independent review identified two substantive risks: it weakened the existing temporal condition and removed the explicit same-topic exclusion. The revised longer wording remains a draft; Claude identified a risk that an unconditional “confirms” may conflate unrelated media repetitions with official confirmation, and potential accidental hardening of unknown times. A candidate closer to the original criterion plus a target-identification procedure is a hypothesis, not reviewed final wording. More audit is not a new source-data census: it evaluates this specific candidate against the signed standard and existing controls, as required before changing what Jev is asked.

AGENTS §7.3 allows small probes costing a few cents in an agreed direction when no stop applies; the existence of uncertainty alone does not require asking again. Registered or full runs and changes that depart from the agreed plan have separate authorization requirements. This council was assigned read-only by its own handoff; that self-imposed constraint must not be attributed to Alex as a user prohibition. No probe has been executed in this retrospective. Product adoption or a change to the existing rules remains a separate decision under the repository instructions.
