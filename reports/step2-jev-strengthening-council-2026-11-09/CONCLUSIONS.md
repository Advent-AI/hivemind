Search correctly found the old H01 candidate; therefore search alone cannot fix that case.

A uniform .5 cutoff is unsupported: on 363 stored merge decisions it blocked one judged wrong and four judged acceptable plus one disputed/overlap; repeated articles make these non-independent. The stricter no-new-detail J2 previously reduced 102 merges to 22, including valid repeats. The retrieval-score diagnostic was interrupted before the decisive errors. The six-pair alternate-model diagnostic mixed model, provider and output format, and is not a comparison of full grouping. None establishes an improvement or the best model. Existing code preserves all candidate revision links and picks only the highest same-statement probability group; the reader can be misled, but the investment impact is not measured.

## Council challenge

Codex and Claude both withdrew their initial recommendation to split the question after checking the actual code and examples. Today the Jev answer is the highest of three choices, with no absolute 0.5 cutoff. Binary questions and a cutoff would be different decisions; `outcome()` cannot accept those raw answers without a mapping, conflict handling, and a way to rank groups. The H01 comparison is mainly between repeat and different, so removing the third label offers no demonstrated fix. Confidence far from 0.5 is not proof of correctness. Claude proposed editing stored inputs as diagnostic counterfactuals; this is a hypothesis requiring review, coherent summary and quotes, reproducible complete request construction and same-day controls, not a fix. A few repeated answers alone cannot prove why Jev made a decision. The final challenge and any response are in `final-challenge.md` / `claude-final.jsonl`; do not assume completion until a result event exists.
