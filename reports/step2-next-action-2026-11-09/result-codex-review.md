# Independent review of the Jev target-identification probe

The candidate did **not** meet the predeclared improvement condition. It did not remove either decisive unsupported relation in C036: both q9 and q7 were `supersedes` 3/3 under the same-day original and 3/3 under the candidate. C057 q1 was `supersedes` 3/3 under the original and 2/3 under the candidate, leaving it only once; the protocol required it to leave `supersedes` at least twice more often in the candidate than control. Stop this candidate under the predeclared rule. The 7 selected requests and 3 repeated sends per arm do not establish a general quality rate, a causal explanation or downstream investment impact.

Other questions' three-label frequency distributions are the same under both arms; numerical probabilities can still differ, and same distributions do not prove equivalent models.

C057 q1 compares the earlier The Information account that NVIDIA backstops unsold GPU capacity in return for a revenue share against the new Futuriom report that Sharon AI joined the program and would purchase up to 40,000 GPUs. Two previous source-only readers judged these main statements different. On candidate draw 0, changing q1 to `same_statement` is therefore **not a correct classification** of that record as a repeat; it merely removes one questionable revision label. The same request's q0 is the earlier record specifically about Sharon's same 40,000-GPU deployment, which is an appropriate repeat; q0's `same_statement` probability .92 exceeds q1's .43 in that draw. D381 consequently chooses q0's group `8:e3` and does not add q1's old `8:e2` revision relation. The existing full outcome accidentally looks better in that draw; it is not evidence that q1 was solved and it would be unsafe to claim that the new question learned to distinguish these two claims. In the other two candidate draws q1 remains `supersedes`, as in all three controls. Three draws cannot establish whether the 1/3 label flip is reproducible or just sampling variation.

## Controls and limitations

- C035 q5: both arms `supersedes` 3/3. The incoming report quotes NVIDIA saying NVL576 would scale on its planned 2027 timeline, addressing the earlier reported NVL576 delay; it does not establish production volume. This is a target recognition control with source limits.
- C073 q10: both arms `supersedes` 3/3. The earlier article reports China locked NVIDIA out to date; the later article reports a planned, conditional H200 opening as sourced to The Information and unnamed people. It is not evidence that the purchases happened or a direct official rule.
- C021 q1: both arms `supersedes` 3/3 for the Kyber report. The earlier article already contained NVIDIA's broad denial. The current request also retains a separate link to the backup assertion; earlier source readers disagreed about its target scope, so this is not an unqualified correct chronology or all-links control.
- B01 q0: both arms `same_statement` 3/3, and all six outcomes choose the same group. The two source articles report Broadcom's AI-chip revenue forecasts of approximately $115bn for FY2027 and $230bn for FY2028; the later one adds other context but does not create a separate forecast. This is a legitimate repeat control.
- C059 q0: both arms `supersedes` 3/3. NVIDIA confirmed shortage while saying it had no future-availability update; the source says it neither confirmed nor denied discontinuation. Whether this is a later same-publisher update remains disputed among prior readers; protocol correctly treats it as observation, not an automatic failure or success.

No supported control in the specified small set visibly regressed, but this does not save the candidate: the decisive improvement condition failed. Do not perform another tuning attempt under the existing predeclared stop. Nor does the result prove Jev can never be improved, that all readers agree on every original relation, or that the unrevised baseline is production-ready. No model call or product change was performed by this independent review.
