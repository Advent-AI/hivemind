**Answer: the falsifier fires. Production does leave kept facts off the questions they bear on, by three mechanisms, but in none of the six
stored production draws did it cost a final answer: every eligible production judgement agrees with reader B, and in the traced cases the
same statement reached the view through other outlets.** The unlinked facts are therefore not the leading repair angle; the losses on the
ruler stay at opening (CENSUS-FULL-RESULT.md, RESULT.md).

## The three mechanisms (fact level, p1–p3's 30 not-linked cases)

1. **No back-filling (11 cases).** Production links a fact only in the call that extracts it: of 3,225 links in the six production draws,
   none points to a view opened after the fact (the clean draws: 2–4% of links, Step 2's later linking). A view opened one article late never
   receives what came before it: p1's Kyber question opened at step 70, one article after the four-die cancellation and the Oberon cover (step
   69) and long after the delay report (step 45); p2's FY2027 revenue question opened at step 102, after the consensus revenue figure had been
   filed under the FY2028 question; p3's data-center revenue question opened at step 115.
2. **Parked under a total-revenue question (6 cases).** With the question that the fact measures open, the fact went to a revenue question
   (D273's fallback sentence, PRD 10 §2's parking lot): "half of data-center spend on memory in 2027" under revenue in p1, p2, p3; Alphabet's
   capex guidance and Google Cloud's backlog under p3's two revenue questions instead of its 2026 capex question; Goldman's AI capex estimate
   under p3's FY2027 revenue.
3. **Filed under a sibling (4 cases, and 3 more hidden behind an article-level link: p3's i179 and i181, p1's i006) or the narrower home by design (4 cases).** Siblings: the four-die cancellation and "roadmap intact" (p3)
   under the 2026 Rubin question while the Kyber question was open, including in the very answer that opened it; the lease backstops and
   the $3.5bn guarantee (p1) under the credit-support total instead of the Ohio question; Goldman's estimate under the 2026 capex question
   (the clean draw c1 files it there too). By design (D207, the narrowest question): the big four's capex under the capex questions, read by
   reader B for revenue (p2, p3); the Rubin shipment schedule under the Rubin question (p3); the Kyber delay under p2's Kyber question
   rather than its FY2028 revenue question.

Five decisive facts were not kept at all though another item of the same article was (p1 i119, p2 i202, i106, p3 i207, i281). The 30
cases: 11 + 6 + 4 + 4 + 5.

## Why none of it cost an answer (the falsifier)

- **Every eligible production judgement agrees with reader B**, in the full run (CENSUS-FULL-RESULT.md) and on the earlier day (RESULT.md:
  "Disagrees among eligible: production 0"). A missing link can only cost an answer on a question that was opened in the ruler's shape.
- **The same statement arrived again.** p1's Kyber question never received the delay report, the cancellation or the cover from their first
  articles, yet received 21 later items from other outlets ("Kyber NVL144 launch has slipped from 2027 to 2028", step 92, and its denial) and
  judged "not by 2027-12-31" from its first judgement (2026-07-06) to the window end, which is reader B's answer for 2027. p3's Kyber question
  received 23 such items. The Ohio view's last judgement (Yes) would agree. The 2027 capex views are eligibility-unresolved and would agree.
- **Where the clean path differs**, it differs by opening its views from the first article (c1–c3's Kyber views at step 45) and by keeping
  unfiled facts unlinked rather than parking them; its later linking back-filled no decisive item to a matched view in this run (the
  "before, linked" cell is 0), so this run gives no evidence that Step 2's back-filling delivers a decisive fact.

## What it leaves

The mechanisms are real and are written for PRD 10's record: no back-filling; the fallback's parking lot; sibling filing. On the ruler, in
this window, they cost nothing, because widely repeated statements re-arrive after a late view opens and every lost answer was lost at
opening. A window where a decisive fact appears once, before its question opens, would show the cost; none of the seven graded questions is
such a case here.
