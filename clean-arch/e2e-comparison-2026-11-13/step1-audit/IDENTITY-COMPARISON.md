## The proposed clause (P), for the council to attack (no file is written yet)

In §2, replace "A different wording, source, forecast or direction alone does not make a new thesis." with:
"A different wording, source, forecast, population or direction is the same thesis; a different quantity, period or decision is another,
when an analyst would bet on each: next year's combined spending is not this year's, and signing an unsigned deal is not the amount of a
programme it may join. A price next year against this year is one thesis, about next year."

In the closing check, replace "A new thesis that asks the same underlying question, about the same subject and scope, as an open thesis or
another new thesis is not proposed" with "A new thesis that asks the same question as an open thesis or another new thesis, by the rule in
section 2, is not proposed".

Sources: §4 "同一题：…另一个估计、来源、措辞、方向；同一总量的另一种人群" and "另一题：…答案能分开走、而且分析员会分别下注的"; §3 D
"同一个量、同一年只有一个问题"; §4 "还没签的单笔交易是'签不签'的是非题，照旧可以开，不并进计划的金额题" (D210); the class-D example. The
examples are written for any industry (combined spending, an unsigned deal, a raw material's price); none names the covered company, its
products or numbers. It permits a separate question; whether to open one still passes the opening tests (council round 7).
