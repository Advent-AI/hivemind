# F6, design version 2 (draft, waiting on the two readers' classification of the 26 declines)

## The sentence, rewritten to separate three things (council round 8)

Before (c8, lines 114–115): "A shared quantity takes its release date and a product event the date its outcome can be checked, only when
the item supports one. A question no date can settle is rewritten or not opened."

After: "A shared quantity needs the year the item supports and a public figure for that year that will settle it (settled_by); state a
release day only when the item gives one, since a later step sets it otherwise. A product event takes the date its outcome can be checked,
only when the item supports one. A question no date can settle is rewritten or not opened."

- **The period** still needs the item's support ("for this or next calendar year"; §4 "原文支持"); a rolling window has no year, so D417's
  decline stands.
- **The settling material** must be named and must publish a figure for that year (§4 "能核对": "有一个日期和一份公开材料").
- **The due day** is not invented: the model states one only when the item gives it, because the ladder step keeps "a day the topic or the
  item states" and otherwise sets the calendar year-end plus 90 days (D414, an estimate that industry data may miss).

## The test (to be fixed after the readers; council round 8's conditions)

- Main probe on fresh articles, preselected by a written rule before any send: items from the stored corpus after the clean window
  (2026-07-15 onward) that no Step 1 measurement has sent, carrying a price or quantity outside any one company with no stated release
  day; a reader marks, before any answer is read, which have a verifiable settling series and which do not.
- Guards (not tuning material): the memory article in D441's states; the capex, Ohio, H100 rental and China-survey articles.
- Denominators fixed in advance: memory and shared-price questions opened per send; of them, those with a year the item supports, a named
  settling series and no invented day; the 2027-against-2026 share counted over all sends, not only over the opened ones.
- The smallest probe that can separate the hypothesis first; a larger test only on a separate council decision.
