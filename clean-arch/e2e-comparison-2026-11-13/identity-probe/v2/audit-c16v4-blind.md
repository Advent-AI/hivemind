**Other sentences in the request.** There is a real tension within the candidate:

- The new next-year example says “another thesis” for the same quantity “beside this year’s,” while the next bullet says a change from this year to next is **one** thesis. “Beside” does not tell the model whether this year is a baseline in one comparison or an independently tracked outcome.
- The separate-question rule requires answers that “can move apart” and an analyst who would bet on each. The existing sentence “If the two cannot move apart, link the evidence” agrees with that requirement. A model treating different years alone as sufficient would bypass it.
- The same-thesis examples agree with “another population or wording of the same aggregate is evidence” and “another estimate, source or wording of what an open thesis tracks.” They do not instruct the model to open a question for another estimate.
