A send with no stored outcome makes the small send unread; it is then sent again whole. A thesis is "tested" in an arm when
at least two of its three sends are ladders of the kind the change is about.

## D416, the typed ruling out (arm typed beside the control)

Read by me, every answer of both arms on the ten theses: the ruling out as written, and the second rung as the judge is
shown it.

- **Does not pass** if any of: an answer of the typed arm writes a wait as the decision (the day arriving, the event not
  having happened, or a decision to go on waiting), on any thesis: the shape was to leave that no place; a send of the
  typed arm is refused twice; a second rung written from the typed parts is not a sentence a judge can read; fewer than
  five of the six product-deadline theses are tested in the typed arm (not proved).
- **Reported, no line:** the waits in the control arm in the same minutes; `by_whom` in every typed answer (a decider, or
  something that is none); the answers with no ruling out in both arms, thesis by thesis; anything on these theses that
  reads worse in the typed arm than in the control.
- **Said beforehand:** the stored rate of the fault is about one answer in eight on these six theses. Eighteen answers
  without it would be expected by chance about one time in ten from the present shape. So a typed arm with none is weak
  evidence that the shape mends the fault; it shows only that the shape did not fail here. The fourth Stage A's 45 events
  are the larger sample.

## D417, the survey rule (arm survey beside the control)

- **Does not pass** if any of: more than one of the six sends of the two survey theses is a ladder; a figure thesis that is
  a ladder in all three control sends is declined in two or three sends of the arm (the rule pulls a year's figure into
  declines); a send is refused twice.
- **Not proved** (the arm is then neither passed nor failed, and no Stage A follows from it) if fewer than four of the six
  figure theses are a ladder in all three control sends: the safeguard for a year's figure would rest on too few controls.
- **Known and left:** a period that is not a year but has a stated publication day is not declined by this sentence, and
  the answer's shape can still carry only a year for a figure. No stored thesis is of that kind; not built for.
- **Reported:** the reason each survey decline gives; u3/clean-2 and u1/clean-8 send by send (theme figures the model has
  sometimes declined).

## D418, the object rule (arm object beside the control)

- **Does not pass** if any of: more than one of the ladders of the three permission theses is under the company; any ladder
  of the three French theses, or of the three company events, is under a theme; a send is refused twice.
- **Not proved** if any of: fewer than two of the three permission theses are tested; fewer than five of the six control
  theses that must stay with the company (the three French, the three company events) have at least two event ladders in
  the arm: declines there would let "none under a theme" pass with nothing read.
- **Reported, no line:** u3/clean-5 (the company's own H200 sales: either object can be argued, its question asks about
  the company's sales); u1/clean-10, which no theme covers and which should stay declined; the control arm's objects in the
  same minutes.
