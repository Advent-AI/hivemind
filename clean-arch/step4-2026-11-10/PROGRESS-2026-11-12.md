## The goal

A system that reads the day's news on a company, opens the questions an analyst would bet on, files evidence under them and
judges each question every day, at least as well as production does on the frozen ruler (AGENTS §7.1). The clean path rebuilds
this in four steps: Step 1 reads an article and proposes theses and evidence; Step 2 groups repeats and revisions and finds the
right thesis; Step 3 writes the library; Step 4 turns each new thesis into a judged question (the "ladder step") and lets
production's frozen judge judge it daily. Production keeps running until the whole clean path beats it end to end (D427).

## How the work has gone (my reading, for the council to challenge)

- **The measuring took more rounds than the building.** The baseline's reading plan needed seven reviews before readers
  started; twice the same finding came back and was closed only by changing the mechanism (every judgement read alone; a
  planted answer used only after a trial). The Stage B build needed three. Each review found real defects (a cap that could
  be passed; controls a reader could pick out), so I do not count them as wasted, but the pace is slow.
- **The readers are the instrument and the instrument moves.** In Stage A the two third readers agreed on 21 of 70 points
  (runs 2, 3) and 29 of 53 (run 4); fresh readers re-reading run 4's answers agreed again on 14 of its 18 agreed faults. In
  the baseline the families agreed on 155 of 159, which shows agreement, not calibration: no control is known to be sound.
- **The ladder step's remaining faults are few but not scattered** (corrected by the council): five of the six answers behind
  lines 1, 3 and 4 sit on theses whose outcome names two things or two periods, which is how Step 1 cut them.
- **Where I was wrong, recorded:** predictions made from one judgement or one skim were wrong several times (a financing
  question I expected to stay unsupported; a "7 of 18 mended" count before the readers); a reviewer started with write access
  once (stopped in a minute, no file changed); a check of mine stricter than the form refused seven correct rows (corrected
  before the count).
