by me in full; `read.py` prints an abbreviation for the tables and is not the reading.

## (a) D420, the proceeding rule (arm proceeding beside the control)

- **Does not pass** if any of: more than one of the nine French answers of the arm has no ruling out, or writes as the
  ruling out the proceeding's going on, a decision to go on with it, or the day arriving; a send is refused twice.
- **Not proved** if fewer than eight of the nine French sends of the arm are event ladders.
- **Reported, no line:** the same count in the control; a French answer whose question is re-cut (to an adverse decision
  only); the four guard events in both arms, each ruling out as written; anything on them that reads worse in the arm.

## (b) D418 narrowed (arm object beside the control)

- **Does not pass** if any of: more than one of the ladders of the three permission theses is under the company; more
  than one of the ladders of u3/clean-5 is under the theme; any ladder of the three French theses or of the three company
  events is under a theme; a send is refused twice.
- **Not proved** if any of: fewer than two of the three permission theses have two ladders; u3/clean-5 has fewer than two
  ladders; fewer than five of the six stay-with-the-company theses have two event ladders.
- **Reported, no line:** the control's objects in the same minutes; u1/clean-10, which should stay declined.
