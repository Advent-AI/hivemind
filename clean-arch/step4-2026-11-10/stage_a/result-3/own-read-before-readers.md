# The third Stage A: my own read of all 132 answers, written before any reader's row

Read thesis by thesis with `stage_a/print_traces.py` (every send's question, outcomes or ranges, reference, due day, what was
left unjudged, what was sent back), 2026-11-10, while the two reader families were reading and before their rows were
collected. What code counts is in `code-counts.txt`. This is one reader, the author of the changes; it is a list of what I
saw, not a count for any line.

## What the changes were built for

- **The favourable end.** All 27 company figures (nine theses) say "lower", three sends each; the reasons name the tie rule
  or the exposure. No company figure is "higher": the two theses that were not amounts owed are a decline (the quarter) and
  a theme's figure in all three sends (acc/clean-10).
- **The due day.** Every year's figure with an estimated day is due on code's day (2027-04-01; 2027-03-31; 2028-03-30). One
  thesis has two due days, and it is an event: u1/clean-6, where one send asks about shipments "in calendar 2027" and sets
  the day at 2028-12-31, a wrong day for that question.
- **The reference.** 15 references are `last_reported` (the 119 billion, the 279 billion, the 25 billion theses, every
  send); the ceiling of 105 billion is no reference in any send of acc/clean-3; the 50 to 60 percent range is spanned by the
  middle range in all three sends of acc/clean-8 (50 to 61, 45 to 65, 50 to 65).
- **What is not a year's figure.** The quarter (acc/clean-5) is declined three times. The surveys: u1/clean-9 declined three
  times (twice for "no theme covers it", once for the rolling period); u2/clean-8 declined twice for the rolling period
  and written once as a figure for "calendar year 2027" under the capital-spending theme: the re-cut the small sends named,
  once in six sends.
