## What the saved evidence shows

- The 49 original saved requests had empty author fields and no source-role fields. The seven experimental prepared-source requests also had empty author fields and did not send the stored source-role classifications. The page text itself can contain a visible name; for example, the prepared Firmus output named its three reporters. These counts do not prove an attribution error. [Reproducible handoff census](../poc/data/clean-step1-ongoing-council-2026-11-07/source-handoff-census.py).
- The current experimental materializer selects article, source, caption and uncertain lines into one text and retains the model's classifications separately. Those classifications are not authoritative: one source role can contain a title, publication and update dates, or an outlet-attributed statement. On article 35, two of three saved selections labeled video-section lines as uncertain; the materializer includes uncertain lines. That is an ambiguity to preserve and test, not proof of a wrong extracted fact. [Role variation](../poc/data/clean-step1-ongoing-council-2026-11-07/role-variation.json).
- A cross-check of 96 saved source-role selections covers 20 previously inspected articles and repeats some articles. The six missing exact located passages in the older ten-page sample were one unrelated DayOne recommendation and five passages from blocks explicitly labeled as AI-generated summaries. The generated-summary facts include details also present in the original articles; an exact match to the generated text does not establish original-body provenance. Six further missing passage instances in the byline comparison are the same unrelated recommendation. This covers only previously cited passages, not all article content or all 49 pages. [Comparisons and limitations](../poc/data/clean-step1-ongoing-council-2026-11-07/role-quote-overlap.json).
- The old cleaner's 112 long-line fallback candidates include both unrelated recommendations and real article content. Twenty-six previously located passage instances are wholly contained in those candidate lines; this includes unwanted material and is not a count of useful evidence. The old cleaner's separate structural extraction can also include unrelated headlines. Simply removing the fallback or trusting all its lines is not a demonstrated solution. [Offline overlap](../poc/data/clean-step1-ongoing-council-2026-11-07/fallback-overlap.json).
- Sixteen saved answers for article 34 reconstruct to the same request body under the current transport's JSON serialization. All omit the Kyber delay report and company denial; five include later results and guidance. The body reconstruction is not a raw HTTP capture. These are repeated answers on one known article over different times, not sixteen independent sources. The omission is established in this example; its mechanism is not. [Reconstructed requests and answers](../poc/data/clean-step1-ongoing-council-2026-11-07/kyber-control-census.json).

The clean runner currently produces an exploratory trace and review page. Its multi-passage output and per-link explanations do not yet have an implemented, verified mapping to the existing evidence writer and readers. Source captures themselves can be incomplete, and page publication time is not a proven arrival sequence. A located quote is not proof that a claim, actor, condition or thesis link is correct. Production readiness requires resolving and testing those interfaces and actual downstream behavior as well as extraction quality.
