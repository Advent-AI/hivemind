# Step 1 reasoning comparison: additional sources

## Result and limits

All 36 requests completed: six manually selected source articles, three draws at each setting, with the same saved exploratory 22-thesis context for both settings. Three independent readers reviewed anonymized outputs against the source before the setting key was opened. The author also checked the source and examples. The six articles are outside the original 49-item clean replay, but prior exposure elsewhere is unknown; selection was not random and article 6 revisits the previously studied Kyber topic. These are observations, not estimates of general error rates. [Replay every article and response](../poc/data/clean-step1-reasoning-new-sources-2026-11-07/review.html).

The outcomes are mixed. Medium preserved some additional useful context, but also produced unsupported links, source attribution concerns and a date-field error. Neither setting is adopted as a quality fix. These isolated calls do not test evolving state, evidence writing, or downstream thesis judgement. The corpus-cleaned input can contain page clutter or omit page content; it differs from the prepared input used in the preceding reasoning comparison.

## Concrete observations

- **Gradium financing:** all six answers correctly say financing reached $100 million in total and NVIDIA joined the investor group; they do not claim NVIDIA contributed the whole amount. The company-reported early revenue appears in two of three low answers and all three medium answers; the missing answer does not establish why the model omitted it. Medium answer-6/e6 quotes a Deepgram product list that is contiguous in the cleaned input but not in the captured original page: the page has intervening product entries. The current clean validator marks it located because this input uses the original cleaned-text path. This is a source-handoff and citation-provenance problem, not an invented quote by the model. Across these 36 answers, 181 of 184 citation instances were located by the clean validator and the resulting text also appears in the captured page; one was marked located but is absent as a continuous passage in the page, and two were unverified by the clean validator. This count checks location, not whether the text fully supports a claim. Two other raw passages differ from the page by a repairable space; their meaning is not thereby wrong.
- **NVIDIA valuation and competition:** all six distinguish FY2027 projections from results and retain the 97% versus 95% *server GPU* shares and customer custom-chip competition. Rising HBM prices are included by one of three low and two of three medium answers; they are potentially relevant context for the existing memory-cost question, not proof that NVIDIA's margins were affected. Medium answer-1/e1 names Bloomberg as `original_party` for Alphabet and Amazon's actions. Medium answer-5 puts relative periods such as “coming quarters” and the share years into `statement_date_text`, although the source does not identify them as dates of the original statements. Medium answer-2/e3 ties the current custom-chip trend to the historical share period more strongly than the article establishes. Low answer-6/e1 may also imply a time overlap; this is an attribution/timing concern, not a confirmed false claim.
- **SK Hynix and supply:** low answer-3 omits the article's supplier demand pressure and prioritization of high-paying AI customers; low answer-6 does not include the source's direct statement that HBM is used in NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra. Whether each deserves a separate record remains an analyst judgement. Medium answer-4 has two unlocated quotations caused by removed spacing; one other passage was repaired. None of this proves an effect on NVIDIA's orders, output or cost.
- **AWS and Nemotron:** all six retain the announced customization capability and the Nano throughput comparison against its predecessor. They describe performance as a vendor claim and do not present availability as customer adoption. Some preserve the tutorial's L40S deployment example and Super product descriptions; omission of unquantified marketing text is not automatically a defect. The captured page names both an AWS and an NVIDIA author, while the cleaned text omits the complete byline; naming the AWS post alone is not proof of an invented source.
- **LG rack prototype:** all six retain the prototype and planned evaluations, orders and production as prospective. Medium answer-3/evidence-1 and medium answer-5/e1–e3 link rack specifications or plans to the existing question about Vera CPU performance and adoption; the article does not establish those CPU outcomes. Low answer-2/e1 classifies an industry-attributed report as an LG party statement; medium answer-5/e2 does likewise for a plan. Medium answer-5 also presents other reported details with a fact tier despite the source's qualified reporting. The other roadmap links can be indirect context, but do not establish an effect on the Kyber setback or Vera Rubin Ultra schedule.
- **Kyber competition:** all six preserve the reported possible 2028 delay, NVIDIA's roadmap response and the author's conditional AMD/Alphabet argument. Medium answer-2/e3 claims a potential AMD opening, but its selected passages omit the article's sentence connecting a delay to both AMD and Alphabet; the quoted AMD paragraphs alone do not make that connection. The source discloses the author's ownership of both competitors; no answer records that disclosure. Its relevance and whether it should be attached require judgement. Exact quotation matching does not establish complete support.

The reviewer-proposed relevance of an omitted item is a lead, not a requirement to create a fixed number of evidence records. Likewise, linking a report as clearly qualified context does not by itself prove that it answers the thesis. The archived answers and their citations are the primary evidence.

## Follow-up: original statement dates

A separate reader examined all 45 non-null `statement_date_text` values across the 124 evidence records, against their article and the Step 1 date rule. Thirteen values across eight answers appear to describe the date of an event or the period of a figure rather than the date of the underlying statement: four in the Bloomberg answer described above; five in three SK Hynix answers (the Friday market debut or the June DRAM market-share point); and four in LG answers where “last month” describes Huang's visit, not a dated statement about the discussion. The article says the chairman was known to have discussed cooperation; the visit date does not establish when that information was stated. Six LG values saying “on the 12th” are ambiguous in context and are not included in the thirteen.

As correct controls, all six SK Hynix answers preserve June for the chairman's explicitly dated statement, and nine Motley records use the article date for the author's own analysis. Eleven AWS values use the publication date for the company's own post; the captured page presents the date in a different format from the ISO metadata, so the distinction between normalized date and date-as-written deserves separate review. These are counts of fields in repeated answers on selected articles, not distinct incidents or a population rate. The reader did not see the low/medium key while performing the review.

Three retrospective continuations on the exact saved requests and answers completed. Asked specifically about the date fields, the model distinguished the forecast period, the date covered by a market-share figure, and the time of a visit from the original statement date; it said the original statement date was unknown in those examples. These leading follow-ups show the distinction in a later response, not why the original answers were wrong or whether a new prompt would fix the class. ## Resource use and next question

Across 18 responses per setting, low used 21,493 completion tokens including 4,637 reasoning tokens; medium used 67,495 completion tokens including 41,778 reasoning tokens. Each setting received 112,011 prompt tokens. The answers contain 48 and 76 evidence records respectively; differences in splitting and context make those counts unsuitable as a quality score. A focused council review is investigating source attribution, unsupported links and complete quotation support against the actual request and previous attempts. No claim is made that a particular instruction caused any error.
