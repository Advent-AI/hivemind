## 5. Tools built this session (all in `clean-arch/e2e-2026-11-09/` unless noted)

- `adapter.py`: replays a clean draw into a view store and judges daily with the frozen judge. It has three modes:
  - seeded (22 seeds);
  - seeded with `--q23`;
  - unseeded mapping mode, `--unseeded --map clean-N=vK[,vJ]`, where all 23 ruler views exist from seq 0.

  It enforces leakage, selection-equality and provenance checks (`step2/inputs.sha256`), and a hard cap through `budget.py`. Its tests
  are in `test_adapter.py`: 15 pass.
- `budget.py`: a hard per-attempt spending limit. Each attempt reserves its worst case under a lock and is charged its cost from tokens,
  or its full reserve when it gives no usage.
- `control_v108.py`: production v108 re-sent on a seeded ledger.
  - `prove` is the $0 byte proof against the 11-03 archive (1018/1018); `live` refuses without a proof.
  - Known label bug: in live mode the log line says "transport reference archive". Live sends through `budget.Budget`.
- `continue_clean_draw.py <draw number | run folder>`: continues a Step 1 run cut by the network.
  - It continues only network cuts, within the draw's remaining limits.
  - It runs Steps 2–3 over the joined segments and stamps them.
- Launchers: `run_clean_draw.sh` (seeded) and `clean-arch/unseeded-2026-11-10/run_unseeded_draw.sh` (empty library).
- `clean-arch/unseeded-2026-11-10/extract_opened.py`: lists a draw's opened theses for the matching read.
