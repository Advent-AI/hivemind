Before you answer, read back each statement, one by one, and write its decision:
- signed_status: "not_yet_signed" when the item describes the obligation as not yet signed (for example proposed, planned, expected or
  under negotiation); "undertaken" when the item says it is already undertaken (already signed, including a deal already signed under a plan tracked by amount;
  a plan or programme as a whole is undertaken once the item reports a deal signed under it, while each single deal within it keeps its
  own status, so an unsigned deal under such a plan is still "not_yet_signed"); "unclear" when the item gives no
  indication either way; "not_an_obligation" when it is not an obligation the company takes on in its own name (including when nothing in the item says the
  company itself would take it on, such as another party's plan or funding target), or it is the company's own borrowing or leases, or
  a completed or unsized investment.
- company_impact: which of the company's own revenue, profit, cash or credit risk the obligation could change, and, when the item states
  its amount, that amount beside 5% of the company's most recent annual profit as the request gives it (D389). Write no figure that neither the item nor the request
  gives. When the item states no amount for the obligation itself, say so and judge qualitatively from what it does state (the cost of a
  project or asset is not the obligation's amount). For a statement that is not an obligation, write "not an obligation".
- large_enough: with a stated amount, true when it is about 5% or more of that profit and false when it is clearly below; with no stated
  amount, the conclusion of your qualitative judgment on whether it is large enough to change the company's revenue, profit, cash or
  credit risk.
- separate_bet: true only when the obligation merits a separate investment judgment.
- settleable: true only when public material (a filing, an announcement) will settle on a date whether it is signed.
- already_asked_by: the ID of a shown thesis that already asks whether this obligation is signed; otherwise null.
- opens: true when signed_status is "not_yet_signed", large_enough, separate_bet and settleable are true, and already_asked_by is
  null; otherwise false.
Return one new signing thesis per obligation whose statements open, and list every opening statement about that obligation under it.
