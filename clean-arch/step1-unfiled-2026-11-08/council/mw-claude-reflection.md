## 4. Where production really is

The pieces work. What no measurement shows yet is **whether the product, on its own questions, gives an analyst views worth having**, and
no measurement we own can show it: the frozen ruler grades the key's shapes, Stage A grades the ladder step's form, Stage B grades support.
The gap to production is therefore not a prompt sentence; it is the **acceptance itself**: a production gate on fresh articles that measures
what the product delivers (are its questions the big bets an analyst would hold; are its judgements supported and right where the outcome is
known), set by Alex. The frozen ruler stays the historical comparison and is not edited (D261).
