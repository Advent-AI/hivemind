2. **The counter and its checks**

For complete, correctly associated inputs, the counter implements the two requested shares: UNSUPPORTED among decided judgements and SUPPORTED among decided last judgements. It reports unresolved verdicts three ways and excludes INSUFFICIENT from the decided denominator. It also enforces the listed numerical stop and void thresholds. [Counter](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/baseline_count.py:227)

It does **not** refuse every incomplete reading the plan describes:

- **Serious — packet sessions and retries are not tied to their answers.** The counter merges all matching answer files before validating rows. It checks that each packet has a list of times, but has no recorded attempt outcome and does not check that a packet’s rows came from its successful session. The made-up “clean” reading itself demonstrates the missing association: all rows are in the packet-1 file. A reading the plan calls incomplete can therefore produce shares. [Counter](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/baseline_count.py:26), [plan](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/BASELINE.md:51)
- **Serious as an enforcement gap — packet contents are not checked against the key and stored runs.** The counter checks packet numbers and extracts E IDs, but does not compare the request, answer, question or day with the keyed judgement. For example, if packet judgement **158** contained a different answer while retaining its number, the counter could treat a reader’s verdict as a catch of planted 1. The inspected trial does not have this error. [Counter](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/baseline_count.py:114)
- **Refinement — the fourteen checks do not prove the share values.** The sound case looks for the words `UNSUPPORTED among decided`, not the expected numerator and denominator; it does not assert the last-judgement share or exercise every void threshold. The calculations themselves read correctly, but the checks support a narrower claim than “each condition tested.” [Checks](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/check_count.py:16)
