1. **What the probes showed.** There are at least three different causes: financial information left without a question, information missed despite an existing suitable question, and small matters intentionally discarded. The model’s later explanation supports a conflict between “do not open a question for one quarter” and tracking the broader financial trajectory. It is evidence of a plausible mechanism, not proof of the model’s original reasoning. [Round log, §§1–2](clean-arch/step1-unfiled-2026-11-08/LOG.md)

2. **What is weaker than it looks.** The unfiled instruction appears three times, so its prominence is a plausible contributor. The second probe did not meet its declared pass criteria. Nor does a reader’s 15-to-6 count establish a recovery rate: on the same control answers, the two reader families counted 15 versus 6 wrongly unfiled items and 2 versus 7 small questions. 3. **Remaining problems, ranked by likely product impact:**
   - **Financial evidence can disappear**, including a major earnings result. That is a product defect even though the sparse, empty starting library and one draw affect its observed frequency. Calling the test miss mostly protocol or chance would dismiss a real loss.
   - **Relevant information can be left unfiled when a suitable question already exists.** The model itself acknowledged examples; their wider frequency and impact remain uncertain.
   - **Unwanted material and poorly chosen questions remain a trade-off.** Test noise was 8 versus the comparison run’s 4; the small-question judgments disagree substantially.
   - **The test result is an uncertain estimate:** it was one draw, previously exposed, and used a sparse library. Some gold-helpful items also conflict with the intended discard rule. These limits do not erase the financial defect. [Earlier record, §16](clean-arch/takeover-2026-11-07/LOG.md), [D209 and D373](docs/DECISIONS-log.md)
