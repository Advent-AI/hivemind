### The two changes

1. **Shared quantities: partly consistent, partly keeps narrower.** The added sentence says another year is not evidence for the existing aggregate, and that this year and next year are two questions when an analyst would bet separately. The latter follows the standard’s one quantity per period and separate-bet tests. But the categorical exclusion of another year as *evidence* has no such basis and conflicts with the request’s own instruction to consider relevant evidence from a related period. The standard distinguishes the identity of a question from the usefulness of evidence; it does not establish that a different year can never inform it. [System lines 102–105](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step1-unfiled-2026-11-08/arms/c14-system.txt:102), [lines 62–70](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step1-unfiled-2026-11-08/arms/c14-system.txt:62); [standard lines 279 and 302](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:279).

2. **Same year in the closing check: consistent in its narrow purpose, but incomplete.** Adding “for a yearly quantity, the same year” prevents two different annual periods from being merged merely because their subject and wording match. It does not itself say that different years must open: the separate-bet and opening tests still apply. However, “same year” leaves fiscal versus calendar-year treatment unstated, and the sentence gives no equivalent rule for the same event under a later deadline. The surrounding check can therefore direct a model to open a later-deadline version while the earlier one is still open. [Closing lines 14–15](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step1-unfiled-2026-11-08/arms/c14-closing.txt:14); [standard lines 345–350](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:345).
