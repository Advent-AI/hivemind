**The 80 sends should wait until the record builder and the comparison rules are made reviewable.** The design identifies the records to compare, but the builder for those records does not yet exist in the supplied directory, and the current marking rules can call an incomplete answer supported.

- **Sample:** The screen selects articles containing *any* revenue or margin figure, not necessarily Nvidia’s. For example, article 24 has TSMC revenue and margin figures; article 27 has Micron margin figures. That is acceptable if disclosed, but it does not ensure the six Nvidia questions are exercisable. Freeze and retain the screen rule and the 81-item population so the sample can be reproduced; do not replace articles after reading results. If fewer than five questions are exercised, the result must be **inconclusive**, not a failure of either record.
- **Constructed state and one draw:** The stored trace has 40 requests against the same seven-question week-end library. That supports a fixed-state comparison, not a forward week. One answer per article cannot separate a change in the request from ordinary variation; report the individual outputs and avoid claiming that one version is generally better. Predefine what happens if either arm has an invalid or missing answer: do not quietly compare different article populations or treat missing output as an empty record.
- **Record builder:** `build_views.py` is named but not present yet. Before a paid send, define and check the exact selection, grouping and merge rules with stored answers and controls. In particular, prevent merged rows from losing different bases, sources, dates or qualifications; preserve a route from each displayed row to its supporting article and passage. Otherwise a reader’s wrong attribution cannot reliably be traced to the record. The two arms must receive the same presentation rules apart from the fields they actually possess.
- **Readers and comparison:** One reader per record confounds the record with the reader; a difference between answers could be either. Treat the comparison as a lead, or have both record readers independently answer from both anonymized views without seeing each other’s answers. For Q2 and Q6, a comparer must check **each required estimate or pair** against the articles, including omissions: an answer that lists one supported pair cannot be marked fully supported merely because that pair is correct. Check each cited row as well as the article, so an answer correct by inference or accident is not credited to the record.
- **Verdict and missing material:** Define how `none` and `incomplete` count when the articles contain a required part, and treat disagreement over whether the articles contain it as unresolved if it could change the line. The current wording exercises a part only when *both* comparers say it exists; without an explicit settlement for that disagreement, a disputed required part could disappear from the score. Preserve open disagreements and do not allow an empty or unidentifiable reference population to pass.
- **Claim:** Even a clean result would describe these screened articles, this constructed library, one draw and the tested readers. It would not show complete extraction, a forward-week outcome, an effect on a judgement, or that the product should display the record.
