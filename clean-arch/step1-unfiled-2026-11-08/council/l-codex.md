**Alex’s distinction is valuable: a thesis needs a reason worth investigating, while a number can tell us whether that reason is playing out.** For example, “memory costs will squeeze margins because the company cannot pass them on” is a view; a reported margin is a fact that helps test it.

Claude’s reading goes too far in three places:

1. **A view need not always be differentiated against the market.** That is a powerful investment question when a trustworthy expectation is available. Alex did not say every view must establish what is priced in, and forcing that test could discard an important emerging risk before consensus exists. Nor should the system invent a causal reason when an article does not provide one.

2. **A separate financial record is a plausible design, not yet a consequence of Alex’s words.** A figure can already be evidence for an existing view. The unresolved case is a useful figure with no view yet: the current design drops it, while the proposed scoreboard would retain it. That is a real product and architecture choice, not merely a new label. [Current Step 1 contract](../../PRD.md)

3. **The case rulings do not define universal financial rules.** Alex dropped the particular cash figures shown to him; they may matter for another company or view. Likewise, he dropped that target price, not necessarily every valuation. Guidance is management’s expectation, not automatically market consensus. [Rulings](../LOG.md)

What would change:

- **Step 2:** matching on measure, period, kind and party is insufficient. A revised forecast can share all four; two reports can describe the same figure with different scope or conditions. Preserve the source and distinguish repeats from revisions using the full statement.
- **Step 4:** “actual versus expected” is useful context, but not automatically the right reference for *every* view. The relevant expectation, date and scope must match the question.
- **D209:** keeping useful figures without a thesis changes today’s drop rule. It needs an explicit design decision; calling them conditions does not settle that conflict.
- **Frozen evaluation:** it stays unchanged and can still report its existing counts, but it was not designed to judge this new record or whether a view has a reason. A separately reviewed evaluation is needed; scores must not be presented as directly comparable. [D209 and D371](../../../docs/DECISIONS-log.md)

D208 is useful history, not prior approval for this design: it described a seeded hierarchy, while the current design retired preset questions. [D208](../../../docs/DECISIONS-log.md)

I would first design and review **one source-backed financial-context record that can also be linked to a view**, including what is kept and how revisions work. The 16-case probe can diagnose it, but cannot establish the new architecture works.
