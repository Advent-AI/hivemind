**BLOCKER.** The naming requirement is gone, but the revised sentence still excludes a whole programme from a signing question whenever its deals feed the tracked total.

D248’s same-number test compares **questions**, not whether a programme’s deals contribute to a total. The signed standard also permits an obligation to be one deal **or one plan**, and tracks an unsigned obligation by whether it is signed. An unsigned material plan could therefore merit its own signing question beside the total.

The instruction to judge each single obligation on its own protects the separate Ohio deal more clearly, but it does not resolve that plan-level exclusion.
