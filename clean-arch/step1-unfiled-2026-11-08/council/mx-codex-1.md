### 5. One test and its falsifier

**Run a read-only, fact-by-fact delivery audit on the stored week.** Preselect unread financial facts from multiple articles, matched linked facts and unrelated controls. For each, record the original passage, date, existing question, what the judgement actually received, and whether the fact could alter or substantiate that answer. Include cases arriving both before and after the question opened. Report duplicates separately and have a second reader challenge relevance.

**Refutation:** if the selected unread facts do not materially inform any existing question beyond evidence already shown, the hypothesis that better delivery to *existing* judgements recovers substantial reader value is unsupported. The audit may instead show a visibility need or strengthen the case for separately tracking annual numbers; it cannot itself prove either change improves judgements.
