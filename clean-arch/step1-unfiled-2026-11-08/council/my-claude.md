## Lessons at the level of mechanism

- **I followed the largest named difference down a chain instead of re-ranking from the goal.** Each sub-question was reasonable
  given the last answer; none was chosen by asking "what would move the choice of system most". After the second step that ended
  in "not separated" I should have stepped back (D466 says so in terms). The council corrected single conclusions round by round;
  it was not asked about the direction until now.
- **Most of the effort went into instruments, and the instruments are loose.** Two reader families differ by about half again on
  "does this bear" (53 against 34 of 63); a finding confirmed by four readers split under two others; a "both readers" rule gives a
  minimum with a wide band. Counts on seven questions, five matters or three sends cannot separate systems that differ modestly.
- **Alex's choices said more about the product than the measurements did.** He kept D382, chose figures-as-evidence (D505), and
  asked to size a gap before changing anything (D507). He was never put the main question in a form he could answer.
- **I repeatedly wrote more than was observed** ("would not have moved the answer", "the new system is ahead", "a thorough reader
  finds all eleven", "small beside 196 links"), each caught by the council. The pattern is one fault: stating the reading I expected
  as a result.
