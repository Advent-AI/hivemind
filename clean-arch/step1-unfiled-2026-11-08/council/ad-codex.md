**One blocking clause remains:** “A single deal, project, partnership or programme … opens no thesis unless it is the company’s own obligation.”

For example, a company’s next-generation product launch can also be described as a **project**. The product-node rule says to track its first shipments, but this sentence tells the model not to open it unless it is an obligation.

The [standard]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:315) applies a size and business-impact test to a single project; it does not categorically exclude one. The smallest fix is to remove the blanket sentence and let the four reasons and the existing “big matters” test decide.
