## 2. Packet shape

A reader cannot identify a control **from its position or packet shape alone**: stored judgements, plants and repeats are all presented individually. That closes the specific problem raised in the second review. Content can still reveal a plant: an obvious contradiction is intentionally visible, and the rewritten previous conclusions in judgements **114** and **154** have unusually close wording to the planted answers.

Reading alone does not remove material that the form permits for the verdict. The form expressly says to judge from the request as sent, which includes the previous conclusion; `not_shown` is used separately for E. It does remove the chance to notice continuity across earlier answers, but that is not material the form says may decide the verdict. [Form](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/ladder/stage-b-reader-brief.md:5)

**The promise to use the same shape in Stage B should go to Alex as a design choice.** The baseline may choose a packet arrangement to carry out the authorized reading, but the design describes reading by **thesis packet**, while the plan commits the later comparison to individual, mixed-question packets. That changes the conditions of the future test, not just this baseline’s packaging. Stage B’s form and lines are expressly to be reviewed and put to Alex before its send. ## 4. The counter

For a complete, correctly keyed reading, the main calculations follow the plan: agreed first-reader verdicts take precedence, agreed third-reader verdicts resolve disputes, INSUFFICIENT is separate, and both shares are printed with the three treatments of unresolved verdicts. The counter checks many of the newly listed stop conditions. [Counter](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/baseline_count.py:134)

It does **not yet refuse every incomplete or void reading the plan lists**:

- **Dropping a failed trial plant cannot work as written.** The plan permits dropping it and using fewer; `POPULATION` requires exactly six. If the number changes, the allowed misses also need recalculating from the design’s “more than one in five,” rather than always allowing one miss. [Plan](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/BASELINE.md:43), [counter](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/baseline_count.py:18)
- **Times need to be finite and non-negative.** Python treats booleans as numbers here; negative values and `NaN` can also pass the numeric check. A negative or `NaN` total can evade the five-hour comparison. The counter also does not establish that recorded values cover a stopped attempt and its retry, rather than just the successful attempt. [Counter](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/baseline_count.py:103)
- **The population check counts entries but does not verify their identities.** A key with 159 stored entries and 16 last flags can still duplicate or misidentify judgements and omit others. Likewise, a repeat without its stored twin can cause an exception rather than the promised named incomplete result. [Counter](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/baseline_count.py:89), [repeat lookup](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/baseline_count.py:158)
- **Some invalid rows still pass.** E accepts arbitrary strings rather than checking IDs against `not_shown`; a SUPPORTED row can say `reasoning_flaw: changes_the_rung`, despite the form saying that value always goes with UNSUPPORTED. Reasons are only checked as nonempty after conversion to text, so a number can pass as a sentence. These defects affect the reported supporting marks and the claimed completeness of the reading. [Form](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/ladder/stage-b-reader-brief.md:44), [validation](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/baseline_count.py:51)
- **A plant missed by both has the wrong prescribed response.** The design says stop until **the form is mended**; the plan says mend **the control**. A bad control and a bad form require different responses. The miss needs diagnosis before either is changed. [Design](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/DESIGN.md:1489), [plan](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/stage_b/BASELINE.md:83)

The **five-hour threshold is a defensible proposed interpretation**, not an exact restatement of the design. The design estimates about 2.3 hours per reader for 159 judgements and says a *pass* over twice its estimate is not measured. The plan adds nine controls, estimates 168 at 51 seconds each, rounds 2.38 hours to 2.5, and applies twice that to the **sum of first-reader session time per family**. That is a reasonable workload budget, but the design also separately estimates third-reader and author work and describes thesis packets. State that this is the baseline’s chosen interpretation rather than claiming the design itself specifies five hours per family. [Design](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/step4-2026-11-10/DESIGN.md:1504)

## 5. Other serious point

The plan appropriately says the results cannot establish reader accuracy. That limitation is especially relevant if the resulting baseline is later used as a pass/fail comparison: the plants test obvious wrong answers but provide no known sound answer, and matching on six plants does not measure the probability of a false failure across the criterion. This is more than wording, though it does not by itself mean a sound-answer control can be invented from an unreviewed stored judgement.

**Not ready — reconcile the failed-plant and missed-plant procedures with the design; make the counter enforce the actual population, valid times and row contracts; establish the plants through the proposed trial while retaining their ambiguity limits; and put the future Stage B packet shape to Alex before treating it as fixed.**
