1. **The standard is appropriate as a product diagnostic.** PRD 10’s test asks whether a fact can change an answer, including through reasoning beyond the fact itself. It does not replace the frozen ruler or impose production’s filing rules on the clean path. The [brief](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/research/2026-11-14-use-acceptance/delivery/brief-needed.md) should make clear that a fact may change the *likelihood or confidence* of an answer without changing the chosen answer immediately. Requiring a definite movement of the final answer risks excluding such evidence.

2. **The reading needs an observable coverage check.** Seven questions against 146 lines is 1,022 question–line opportunities for each reader. Give readers manageable question-by-question packets, retain independent readings, and require an explicit completion or “none” for each question. Otherwise an omission is indistinguishable from a considered rejection. The table alone also carries known source errors, including T-114 and T-115; an agreed candidate needs its article and stored passage checked before its source and relevance are reported.

3. **Compare the actual figure and claim in the request.** A same-claim group may contain a statement with several figures, only some of which appear in the shown statement. Check the value, measure, period, source and qualification that make the figure relevant; use the group only to locate candidates. Also distinguish “shown at least once” from delivery to a particular judgement: a later appearance does not establish that an earlier eligible request received it. Respect what was known on each request’s date.

4. **“No agreed finding” is not necessarily “no gap.”** One family’s candidate may be a real omission. If the readers disagree, a packet is incomplete, or the hand check cannot settle the claim, report **undecided** and the leads; do not invoke Alex’s “none, stop” branch as though absence were established. The delivered items are a useful check on the readers’ bar, but a link itself does not prove relevance.

5. **The report must not claim** that an omitted figure would have changed a judgement, that all useful evidence reached the seven questions, or that this test chooses between the systems. A confirmed finding establishes a relevant kept figure absent from the specified requests, within this one run and this financial-table scope.
