**The proof is close, but I would not yet call the registered proof passed.** The new tests support the reported case-specific results, including the synthetic closure and the later article waiting behind the failed one. One of the registered checks is still broader than its design permits.

[The design](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/research/2026-11-14-use-acceptance/duplicates/D375-PROOF-DESIGN.md) says that any difference outside an arm’s allowed differences fails it. [The script](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/research/2026-11-14-use-acceptance/duplicates/d375_proof.py:34) instead allows whole tables to differ. For example, the “same” arm permits differences in `evidence_links`, while its end-state check examines the tested article’s links. An unrelated changed link in that table could pass. Make the check compare the actual changed rows and values against the declared differences, including the expected removals and additions. Add a mutant that changes an unrelated row **inside an allowed table**; it must fail.

Also make each recovery check require its intended interruption and expected failure record, rather than merely allowing those records. Then the result can be called a **passed, bounded serial proof**, with the existing limits: fake answers, the tested cases, and parents unexercised because none existed in the four runs. A nonempty synthetic parent case should be part of a real implementation’s verification; it need not hold up this narrowly labelled proof if the limitation stays explicit.

For the measurement design, **do not send the model a list described as the start-of-day list when it includes questions opened later that day**. Correcting that wording and changing the list are one coherent intervention: the request must truthfully describe its input. Audit the whole new request against the standard, preserve the frozen prompt and configuration, and compare the new version with the unchanged default. A separate wording-only arm is not needed merely to count levers.

I would design the **smallest end-to-end test that can answer a product question first**, rather than commit now to three full July draws:

- Use complete consecutive days containing independently identified same-day openings, preserving the preceding state and running the normal downstream path through the final judgement. Do not select only individual changed articles and pretend their later state is unchanged.
- Re-send a **same-day default control**, with repeated draws sized from the actual cases and cost. The three stored July draws are valuable baselines and design material, but PRD 10 §6 explicitly says not to use archived answers as the control because the model can drift. [PRD 10](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/10-filing-phase.md:120)
- Before choosing the window, use the stored runs to identify and read cases where a fact could reach an earlier same-day question and where a wrong or broader filing could result. If there is no eligible case with a final judgement to inspect, the small test is **unexercised**, not a pass. The July frozen ruler remains unchanged, but its final-question counts must not be described as a direct measure of correct filing.
- Register a prediction about **facts received by the relevant final judgement**, with source-backed cases and a harm control for wrongly filed or lost facts. Read every consequential changed case against the article, both requests and final answers. Report duplicate questions and no-home statements as supporting observations, not the product outcome. A changed answer is not automatically a better answer.
- Stop if the expected case is not exercised, the necessary fact still does not reach its judgement, or the change introduces a consequential wrong filing or loss under the prewritten rule. If results are mixed or within control variation, report them as inconclusive; do not claim no effect.

Serial is appropriate for that first test, with parallel recovery and acceptance still open. It is not correct to promise “the same answers by design”: model answers vary, and changed earlier outputs can alter later requests and state. Price the **complete proposed control and changed paths**, including downstream comparison and judging, from their actual requests and token records; the three-to-four-dollar figure is not yet a budget.
