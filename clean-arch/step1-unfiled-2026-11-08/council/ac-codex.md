1. **Changed clauses against the standard**

- **Opening test and four reasons — consistent with the requested scope**, including the explicit exclusion of class A. The standard requires a meaningful, independently trackable question that public material can resolve ([§3–4, lines 277–280, 308–310]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:277)).
- **Product node — keeps narrower:** “single product … opens no thesis unless” it is an obligation contradicts the product-node rule and its separately bettable milestone exception ([lines 277, 349]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:277)).
- **Obligation — keeps narrower:** “guaranteeing or backing” is less explicit than credit support, and the only example asks whether an obligation will be signed. The standard also opens the **amount already undertaken**, checked in the next annual report ([line 278]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:278)).
- **Shared quantity and policy stage — consistent** in their basic definitions ([lines 279–280]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:279)).
- **Small matters and separate bets — consistent**, but the blanket prohibition on individual deals, products, projects and programmes **keeps narrower** than the standard’s size test ([lines 310, 315]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:310)).
- **“Do not invent a decision date” — contradicts** the allowed analyst-set check dates for undated pending regulatory decisions and unsigned obligations ([lines 280, 320–323]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:280)).
- **“A year after next” — keeps narrower:** the standard excludes later **annual quantities**, with an obligation exception; it does not ban all later events ([lines 339–340]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:339)). Other changed exclusions broadly follow §4, while treating own borrowing and leases as categorically ineligible **keeps narrower** than the standard’s qualification that they can be assessed under another reason ([line 337]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:337)).
- **`opens_as` — consistent in scope**, subject to the product restriction above. **`settled_by` — consistent** as a public-material field ([lines 274–280]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:274)).
- **Closing check — contradicts** the permitted analyst-set dates and can apply the overbroad bans. Its closest-question test also gives no clear pass when the open list is empty; the schema explicitly permits `null` then.

2. **Five requested cases**

The obligation amount is at risk from the signing-only example and later-year ban. The rack date is at risk from both blanket bans. Combined hyperscaler spending, DRAM contract prices and chip-import approval fit their stated categories, but remain vulnerable to the date and closest-question instructions. These are reading-based risks, not observed send results.

3. **Examples and linking**

The insurer guarantee and five-cloud-provider spending examples reproduce the standard’s benchmark examples closely; they are bench-fitted. The examples span industries overall. Section 2 and the closing rows require links to **every** informed question, but section 3 says to link to **the** closest one when the check fails; that can wrongly omit other valid links.

4. **Smallest fixes**

Restore the standard’s product-milestone and obligation-amount language; limit the later-year ban to annual quantities with its obligation exception; allow the specified analyst-set check dates; explicitly pass the closest-question check when no question is open; and link retained evidence to every relevant question. Replace the two copied examples with general examples from other domains.
