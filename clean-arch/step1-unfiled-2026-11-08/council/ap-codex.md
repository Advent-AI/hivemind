5. **Lessons to keep**

Preserve each reader’s actual verdict when counting; labels such as “overlap” cannot erase an explicit rejection. Count missing items in the complete-system view as well as the search-only view. Test whether grouped information is usable, not merely stored. Finally, distinguish a link that predicts an exact outcome from information that helps an analyst understand its risks.
