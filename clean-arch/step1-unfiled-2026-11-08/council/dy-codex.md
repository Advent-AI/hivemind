**BLOCKER.** The cited standard says how to track a plan **once undertaken**. D388 says how to treat a signed deal under a plan tracked by amount. Neither says that one signed deal makes the **whole plan** undertaken.

For example, a company could sign a pilot deal while a larger programme remains subject to a separate agreement. The new sentence would mark the whole programme undertaken and prevent its signing question.

The wording does explicitly preserve Ohio’s individual unsigned status.
