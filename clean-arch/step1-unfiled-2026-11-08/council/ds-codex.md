The translation of the quoted sentence is faithful, but the new rule is **narrower than D248 and D388**.

D248 says different descriptions checked against the same number are one question. It also keeps independently changing numbers separate. It does not say every programme has only one total, or that a material unsigned deal must name a counterparty or asset. The new rule adds both limits.

Ohio itself names OpenAI and the lease, so it is not excluded by that condition. But a statement describing both a programme and its Ohio deal could be rejected as a programme statement. Fresh-8’s acquisition is named; its existing completion question and the deal’s signing status still need to be judged separately.
