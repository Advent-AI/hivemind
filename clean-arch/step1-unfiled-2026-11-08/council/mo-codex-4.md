**Not ready for the slice:** the new code closes most of the original findings, but two launcher-failure paths can still let another process send after a crash.

1. **The stale-launcher check and relaunch lock are not atomic.** [run_fresh.py](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/e2e-comparison-2026-11-13/run_fresh.py:176) checks registrations, releases the ledger lock, and only then takes `launch.lock`. If the old launcher is alive during the check but dies before the new launcher takes that lock, the new launcher can acquire it. Until it records the old attempt as crashed, another process’s gate sees the lock held and treats the stale registration as live. It can admit a send during that interval. The [test](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/e2e-comparison-2026-11-13/test_run_fresh.py:192) covers a launcher already dead before the check, not death between the check and lock acquisition. **Smallest fix:** hold the ledger lock across the stale check and nonblocking launch-lock acquisition, then register or refuse before releasing it. Test that exact interleaving.

2. **An unexpected launcher exception removes the evidence of a crash.** [run_fresh.py](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/e2e-comparison-2026-11-13/run_fresh.py:189) unregisters the launcher in `finally` even if an unexpected exception escapes. For example, after `Popen` starts a child, saving its PID at [line 294](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/e2e-comparison-2026-11-13/run_fresh.py:294) can fail. The child can remain alive, the registration is removed, and its gate sees no dead launcher; other draws can send too. The test that raises `RuntimeError` checks recovery on the *same label’s later relaunch*, but does not check the shared stop before that relaunch. **Smallest fix:** unregister only after a normal terminal state or after a crash has stopped the ledger and the child has been ended. On an unexpected exception, stop the ledger and terminate the child, or leave the registration for the gate to detect.
