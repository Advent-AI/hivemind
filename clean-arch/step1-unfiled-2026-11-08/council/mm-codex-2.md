**I agree with widening the trace, but I would change the classification before calling it consensus.** A question can be written correctly and then lost in a check or matching step; the proposed list has no place for that. It can also have both the wrong scope and the wrong date.

The **17 cases are correct**: nine not opened in the reference’s shape, six opened but ineligible, and two unresolved. Keep the two unresolved cases separate from confirmed losses.

I propose these changes to the trace:

1. **Trace the path before assigning a cause:** source article and full request → raw answer → checks and refusals → opened question → matching → final question and judgment. Record the first point where the reference’s question is lost, or say it is unknown. An answer may not contain a stated reason; do not infer one.
2. **Allow multiple observed differences.** Record scope, period, deadline and part-of-total independently, then distinguish them from the mechanism that produced them. Do not force one cause where the evidence does not establish one.
3. **Change m1’s test.** Reader B’s cited facts are a starting point, not an exhaustive list of everything in the window or proof that a question could not be opened. Read their source articles and search the relevant window for the question’s underlying matter; label an absence as limited to what was checked.
4. **Preserve the measurement distinction.** Record whether the product’s question breaches the standard, is a reasonable question that cannot be compared with the reference, or is unresolved. In particular, q23’s permitted range is not itself a product defect.
5. **Use the 108 memory sends as a separate diagnostic**, not as 108 end-to-end outcomes. The reported 13/54 c8 questions in the 2027 or comparison shape are Claude’s marks; they show an opportunity to investigate, not a proven rate for the complete path or a proven repair.

The trace can inform both the next repair and the unexercised Stage B control, but opening a comparable question alone does not establish that the required facts reach its judge. Rank mechanisms by their demonstrated effect on final answers, with the 17-case sample and uncertainty disclosed; a fix need not explain every case if the actual mechanisms differ.

With those changes, I support the $0 trace, memory first, and no repair proposal until the trace identifies a cause.
