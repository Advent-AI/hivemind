# Step 1 source provenance: missing generated-summary labels

## Confirmed source-preparation mechanism

Two NVIDIA technical pages in the preregistered comparison contain an explicitly labeled AI-generated summary in the captured page and original HTML. Their cleaned `text` retains summary sentences but not the label. The Step 1 request sends the cleaned text, so Luna cannot tell from that text that those sentences came from the generated-summary component. The body may independently support some of the same statements; the missing label does not make every extracted statement false.

The saved cleaner first removes common site-template lines and short fragments in [`clean_pages.py`](../poc/corpus/scripts/clean_pages.py). It then retains sufficiently long sentence-like rule-cleaned lines even when the article extractor omits them. It does not re-add lines removed by the first pass. On the two inspected NVIDIA pages, the repeated short label is removed while four longer summary lines survive that fallback; the original functions reproduce the stored cleaned text. This is a source-handoff provenance defect. Turning off the long-line retention fallback is not a solution: prior review found it also recovers genuine article content such as financial guidance.

## Bounded corpus census

The [reproducible census](../poc/data/clean-step1-source-provenance-2026-11-07/census.py) reads all 34,084 saved corpus rows and matches three explicitly bounded page structures. It counts retained exact flattened-page lines and checks known positive controls. It does not decide whether a claim is useful or correct.

| Explicit page component | Pages with missing label and retained component content | Component lines in captured pages | Exact lines retained in cleaned text |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| NVIDIA `AI-Generated Summary`, bounded by `Next Steps` and a later NVIDIA Nemotron disclosure | 149 | 759 | 748 |
| TradingKey `Overview Key Points` followed by `AI-generated summary` | 12 | 12 | 12 |
| Cisco `Summary is AI-generated, editor-reviewed.` and the following three takeaway lines | 2 | 6 | 2 |
| **Total for these three structures** | **163** | **777** | **762** |

These are source records with an identifiable label/content mismatch, not 163 model errors or a system-wide rate. The census does not characterize other label formats, incompletely captured pages, content that was reflowed into different lines, or every source publisher. Another 54 TradingView pages have an AI-related disclosure, but their content boundaries have not been established and they are excluded from this count. The Cisco boundary is intentionally limited to the three observed takeaway lines. Within these bounded components, none of the 762 retained exact lines occurs more than once as an identical whole line in its captured page. This does not rule out paraphrases, partial overlap or ambiguity elsewhere. The archived HTML was independently checked for one page from each of the three structures; that does not establish HTML availability for all 163 pages. The original captured HTML can contain richer structure than flattened `text_page`; availability and quality vary by source.

## Handling and open question

The preregistered replay flags the two known affected pages for the human reader and preserves the original answers; no source content is removed. Across the 163 bounded affected records, cleaned text totals 2,281,395 characters and captured page text totals 2,997,581 characters; median lengths are 14,372 and 18,543 characters. These are character counts for this selected class, not token, cost or latency estimates. Sending the full page would retain labels in these captures but can also expose navigation, unrelated stories, stale widgets and flattened ambiguity; it has not been validated as a general solution. A general solution needs to preserve a source's own provenance labels together with the content they qualify, including labels that precede or follow their content. It must also retain useful article text, source order and separately identifiable quotations; unknown or ambiguous boundaries must remain visible rather than being asserted as verified. The earlier model-proposed source-role selector reduced particular contamination cases but also misclassified some introductions and metadata, so its output is not a general solution.

Codex and Claude Code are reviewing the existing cleaner, source captures and downstream request together before any implementation. No database, frozen production configuration or model prompt has been modified to address this issue. See the [source-handoff council](STEP1-SOURCE-HANDOFF-COUNCIL.md) and the [additional-source replay](../poc/data/clean-step1-date-unseen-2026-11-07/review.html).

## Offline source-lineage replay

The [lineage replay](../poc/data/clean-step1-source-lineage-2026-11-07/review.html) covers the 163 bounded affected records and 200 non-overlapping feed articles selected by a recorded hash seed. An independent reader reran the actual current cleaner and verified the raw and cleaned records: all 363 outputs matched the saved cleaned text. Current HTML was available for 362 pages; the remaining page reproduced through the cleaner's existing no-HTML fallback. Current database and HTML-index hashes remained unchanged during the run. This establishes reproduction with the current saved inputs and extractor, not independent proof that every historical HTML byte is unchanged.

Across 15,280 cleaned lines, 15,026 have one exact whole-line candidate in the captured page, 237 have multiple exact candidates, and 17 have a transformed joined-byline candidate. A single exact text candidate is a location observation; multiple candidates do not reveal which occurrence was retained, and a joined line is not a verbatim original line. The [raw lineage records](../poc/data/clean-step1-source-lineage-2026-11-07/lineage.jsonl) preserve the page and cleaned text, candidate positions, raw character offsets and extractor observations. A known citation is contiguous in cleaned text but not in the captured page; both sets of exact offsets are retained without changing the evidence.

Among the 762 retained lines in the bounded components, 746 NVIDIA lines and two Cisco lines did not meet the cleaner's normalized extractor-text match and had its long-sentence shape; with a nonempty extractor result, that is consistent with the existing long-line retention fallback. Twelve TradingKey lines met the cleaner's extractor-text match. An extractor match checks normalized text, not authorship or semantic provenance. The nearest identified label was one nonempty captured-page line away on 158 affected pages, two on four and three on one. Distance is not evidence of a label's scope, and these three explicit structures do not cover arbitrary publishers. [Reproducible analysis](../poc/data/clean-step1-source-lineage-2026-11-07/analysis.json).

The 200 additional selected articles contain 1,933,930 captured-page characters and 923,342 cleaned-text characters. Across these pages, the median captured page has 168.5 nonempty line positions without an exact or joined-byline candidate in the cleaned output. This is an [observation of mapping and size](../poc/data/clean-step1-source-lineage-2026-11-07/context-sizes.json), not a count of irrelevant or definitely deleted lines; content may be transformed or reflowed. It does not measure tokens or model performance.

This probe does not select or suppress content for Step 1, classify passages, alter the prompt or add a production handoff. The next design decision is how much original page context the extractor should receive without misrepresenting its scope or overwhelming it with unrelated material; a controlled comparison and source-backed review are required.
