# Step 1 council: source data and model processing

**Conclusion:** fix the confirmed loss of test-source content first while establishing a fixed, source-reviewed input for interpreting model behavior. These activities address different questions and can proceed together. Preserving source text is not enough to establish correct article identity or a faithful model answer.

Codex and Claude Code (`claude-fable-5-1`) reviewed the 102-answer full-page comparison, earlier source-selection and prompt attempts, and the saved cleaner and runner code. The council challenged the distinction between model errors and missing, mixed or misidentified source content, corrected unsupported claims, and examined a source-preserving test version. The [round 2](../poc/data/clean-step1-full-page-diagnostic-2026-11-07/council-round2-response.md), [round 3](../poc/data/clean-step1-full-page-diagnostic-2026-11-07/council-round3-response.md), [round 4](../poc/data/clean-step1-full-page-diagnostic-2026-11-07/council-round4-response.md), [round 5](../poc/data/clean-step1-full-page-diagnostic-2026-11-07/council-round5-response.md) and [round 6](../poc/data/clean-step1-full-page-diagnostic-2026-11-07/council-round6-response.md) responses retain the discussion, including corrections and open disagreements. Independent readers checked the captured pages, model outputs, cleaner, candidate mapping and runner contracts.

## Established findings

- The cleaner deleted a CFO's outlook qualification and a separate financing paragraph before the model could read them; it also supplied an unrelated later-story teaser without its identifying context. In selected cases, full-page input made the missing material available and avoided that teaser's misattribution. This is evidence of source preparation failures, not an estimate of all losses.
- The model sometimes changes meaning even when the relevant source sentence is present and cited: five of six selected FEV answers turned an example model into a definite selection. Other cases have attribution, omission or thesis-link concerns. Those errors do not prove all inputs have the same effect, and a correct quote does not prove a correct summary.
- The cleaner switched off 615 of 6,463 upstream-fed articles. One genuine 9,874-character article was excluded because its body was one line; some other excluded captures are wrong pages, paywalls or code. A full-corpus offline probe of a sentence-count replacement would enable 89 and disable 49; independent reviews found usable articles among the latter and unrelated pages among the former. Neither a replacement nor a union of text-shape rules establishes article identity.
- Prior source-role selection, stronger summary wording, higher reasoning and quote-first attempts produced mixed results and were not adopted. The full-page comparison is selected and uses October captures of July articles; its observations cannot establish general accuracy or publication-time completeness.

## Agreed direction and current implementation

Alex confirmed that the test-source data and cleaner may be corrected when they interfere with testing. A separately versioned [source-preservation candidate](STEP1-SOURCE-PRESERVATION-DESIGN.md) now keeps the old clean text and the captured page, retains original and cleaned feed/coverage values, and exposes the saved page as the test input for upstream-fed articles. The new database preserves source identity and completeness as **unassessed**. This addresses availability of saved text in an isolated test path; it does not prove that the saved page is the correct or complete article.

The actual runner received a previously excluded article's page in an offline check using a fake answer. Full-row mapping was checked for all 34,084 items. The original snapshots and frozen production configuration remain unchanged. The replay renderer now displays the candidate's saved representation, previous cleaned input and unassessed source identity; no new model replay is implied. A complete source-quality comparison and the model's behavior on wrong pages and other negative controls remain open. The 271 previously empty clean inputs are available as captured pages in the candidate, but that does not prove their pages are valid target articles. Current runner preselection and stop-on-failure behavior remain unchanged.

## Remaining questions

1. How should target article, generated summary, related material, paywall and capture-time uncertainty be represented without discarding useful content or promoting an unrelated page to a fact? Neither a full page nor a numerical length threshold answers this.
2. With source identity and the article's available text reviewed and fixed, which semantic, attribution, coverage and linking errors persist across repeated model outputs? Reuse prior traces where they answer the question before sending another model request.
3. How will the actual writer and downstream reader preserve multiple source passages and their provenance? The isolated candidate and fake-runner check do not demonstrate that path.

The next test must compare actual sent inputs and complete outputs against source-backed positive and negative controls, preserve genuine uncertainty, and report omissions, incorrect source attribution and model meaning errors separately. No new threshold or production-readiness claim follows from this council.
