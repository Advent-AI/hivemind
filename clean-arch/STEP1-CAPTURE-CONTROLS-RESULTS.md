The two CNBC answers also omitted the article's discussion of how chip residual value and NVIDIA's product roadmap affect the backstop risk. Whether this should be a separate evidence item or part of another matter needs judgment. The page answer's word “secured” may imply a legal security interest more specific than the source establishes; this is an interpretation concern. The cleaned answer's specific attribution and original date for some Wall Street Journal terms also need care. The captured CNBC page was retrieved months after publication, so its completeness and historical version remain uncertain.

Across the five answers, all 31 returned passage instances had a source slice located in both the sent input and the saved captured page, with no lookup errors. This is a text-location observation: the CNBC and Digitimes examples show that a located passage can still leave a summary detail unsupported by the attached quotations. The comparison is one draw per input, does not demonstrate reliability, and cannot distinguish page effects from ordinary output variation.
