## Did we measure wrong?
No. The reading measured what it was built to measure, which is the products as configured (D667).
- **The matter list is independent of both systems.** It was built by blind readers from the 410 kept articles; neither system's
  output went into it.

## Why the new system loses: two mechanisms, both known before the run

**1. By design, D382, D384 and D505: the company's own figures are not questions.**
- 7 of the 32 matters are company figures, and the old system serves 5–6 of them.
- D667 itself says: "The new system does not open such questions by D505, and Alex sees how much of any gap that makes."
- The week of 2026-09-22 to 09-28 (D489) had already shown the same thing: production 4 of 5 big matters, clean 2, for this cause.

**2. A broken hand-off between Step 1 and the ladder.**
- Step 1 opens compound questions: two years in one question, or several figures or outcomes in one. The frozen ladder refuses every one
  of them as "no ladder".
- Those questions never reach the judge and are absent from the library.
- new-2 lost 27 of 60 opened questions this way; new-3 lost 19 of 60.
- Among the lost are matters the old system wins on:
  - hyperscaler capex, M04, opened in both new draws;
  - the Anthropic anchor investment, M17, in both;
  - the Hugging Face acquisition, M19, in new-2;
  - DRAM contract prices, M16, in both.
- This class was identified on 2026-11-12 (D432). The Step 1 versions tried against it (c14, c15yc) did not pass their gates, so it was
  never fixed.

Together the two mechanisms account for essentially all of b, the old-only matters (7–8): 5 company figures, M04 and M17, and M19 in
one draw.

- The pass line is a lead: a − b ≥ 5 and a ≥ 2b. Under D382 the old system serves 5–6 figure matters the new one never can, so the new
  system would need about 12 matters only it serves.
- The matters its rules allow and the old one misses number about 12.
- Even with the no-ladder loss fixed, the new system reaches about 7–8 against 10, which is no winner or a loss, never a lead.
- **This could have been computed in minutes**, from the frozen list (7 figures flagged), D505, D432 and the D489 week, before any paid
  send. AGENTS §7.2 asks for the upside of a step done perfectly to be measured before a phase targets it.
- Neither the council (D665–D667) nor I computed it. Two days and about $145 measured a result that was knowable.
- I added to the waste: a full Step 1 redo (about $21, 17 h) where a re-send of one item ($0.12) was enough.
