The relevant instruction was:

> “Reject everything else, and reject these even when a named party states them: a product feature, performance, compatibility, retail price or content detail that gives no business scale …; an integration of the company's software, models or tools into others' products or research, or a research or award result, that gives no business scale …”

I treated DeepSeek’s chip-development report as an unscaled competitive development. That was a mistake: the instructions also say to keep a competitor’s action with a name that bears on the company’s competition or products. The reported development of DeepSeek’s own inference chip should have been included as evidence.
