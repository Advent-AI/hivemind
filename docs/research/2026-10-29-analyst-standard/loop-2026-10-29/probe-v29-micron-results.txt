I left Micron’s results out because I treated the stated subject list—NVDA—as limiting evidence to Nvidia-related statements. The instruction that led to that was: **“You are a senior hedge-fund analyst reading one item for the subjects it concerns ([Subjects]); ‘the company’ below is a subject company.”** I read “subjects it concerns” too narrowly, as excluding results for another company.

The instruction that would have kept Micron’s results is the evidence rule to **“Go through the item from its first paragraph to its last and take every statement of these kinds; a page with eight such statements yields eight evidence items, not a selection. Stop only at the end of the item.”** Micron’s reported third-quarter revenue and gross margin are published actual figures, and its revenue growth is also a material figure reported in the item. I should have included them as evidence, with the article as the reporting source.
