## Stored conversation accounts (not ground truth)

The model's three continuations were recorded under `poc/data/parallel-refinement-model-accounts-2026-11-05/`, request SHA `d3be2e5e…`; these are its retrospective accounts, not causal proof.

2. NVIDIA Q3: it said v2 takes the $108bn forecast but that the no-China-sales assumption was not a direct topic of v2. That omission is a request defect; whether restoring the detail changes this case is a hypothesis, not proven cause. The system already mentions conditions and indirect connection.

The prior independent review flagged that as risky, but the user's D369 permits indirect evidence with a clear rationale. A speculative claim is not automatically an invalid link; the exact attribution and uncertainty must be preserved. A proposed blanket prohibition would change the standard and is not added.

The model's continued-conversation account under `poc/data/parallel-b-date-account-2026-11-05-v1/` acknowledges no original date was supplied and its earlier date was unsupported.
