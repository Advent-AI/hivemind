# Independent review of the 60-call B diagnostic

The Q3 assumption survived **with** the $108 billion guidance in two of the three treatment answers. It survived only as a separate record in one control answer. The treatment also introduced material regressions: one answer lost quarterly profit, another lost important Rubin Ultra context and the ambiguity in NVIDIA’s response, and several added statements were linked to questions they do not substantiate.

For example, the source says NVIDIA’s Q3 forecast assumes **no China compute sales**. Treatment draws 2 and 3 carry that condition in the same record as the $108 billion forecast. Treatment draw 1 omits it. Control draw 1 records it separately; control draws 2 and 3 omit it. My earlier progress update incorrectly said all controls omitted it.

I read the ten source-and-answer packets, their shown questions, the signed standard and D369/D371, and both source maps and their corrections. The maps informed the review; the judgments below are mine. **C1–C3** mean control draws and **T1–T3** treatment draws. Where an answer ID starts with the article’s full `gnews:` ID, I use its suffix below; an unsuffixed ID is written in full.

- **01 — Microsoft, `gnews:d144b91986c520ea36e718cf`.** All six answers retain the unchanged data-center spending guidance: C1 `e1`; C2–C3 `-1`; T1–T3 `1`. C1 calls the unspecified spending period **2026**, which the source does not establish.
