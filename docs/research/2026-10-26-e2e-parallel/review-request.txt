The contract: an item's accepted extraction must answer the exact request built from the ledger committed by the items before it (serial semantics). Early requests may be sent; only a byte-identical request at the item's turn may be accepted. Only the coordinator thread touches the ledger connection. Failure of an earlier item must accept no later handover or write. The default replay remains serial.
