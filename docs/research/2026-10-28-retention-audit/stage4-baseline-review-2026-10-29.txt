**The design is not ready to send as a scored comparison.** It can become a useful diagnostic probe, but the request and scoring rules need to be fixed first.

The clearest example is Sharon: the incoming statement is called `e6`, and an unrelated historical statement is also called `e6`. A model could cite `e6` and the reviewer could not tell which statement it meant without an explicit identity rule.

## Serious issues

1. **The exact request is not yet defined, and historical answers can leak into it.** The draft says to use the complete source, extraction and historical context, but does not specify the fields sent or how the target is identified among other extractions. The case file also contains `status: dropped`, `drop_reason: no home`, final links and the previous placement answer. Sending the case object wholesale would reveal the old result. Conversely, the other extractions are embedded in the old placement request, which also contains historical instructions and truncated quotes. Hashing a request later will preserve whichever choice was made; it does not decide the choice. Define and inspect the exact model-visible payload and exclude outcome labels before sending. The old checker accepts the incoming ID as an argument citation while also accepting IDs in the historical expansion, so a citation can pass validation while referring to the wrong record. Give the incoming record an unambiguous identity in the research request and scoring contract. See [Sharon incoming](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/stage4-user-case-context-2026-10-29.json:10), [historical `e6`](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/stage4-user-case-context-2026-10-29.json:713), and [prototype checks](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-27-stage34-gold/run.py:193).

3. **The score is not fixed tightly enough to prevent drift beyond Alex’s rulings.** The table correctly leaves Sharon’s exact destination and NVL72x2’s page open. But it also asks a model reviewer to determine investment classification, erroneous placement and no-home handling without a fixed set of outcomes or examples for those judgments. For example, Sharon being a supply/demand clue does not by itself establish whether a particular finance page may use it as context. A plausible answer could be scored differently by different reviewers. An `unresolved` answer, or `background_only` without a suitable ID, does not alone establish why the model made that choice. Record these as contract or evidence limitations when the text does not support a conclusion; do not infer a correct economic judgment from the action alone. Source independence also needs actual provenance, not merely a different outlet name.
