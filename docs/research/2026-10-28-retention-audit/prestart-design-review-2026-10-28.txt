The design has four serious gaps. For example, an item can count as successfully placed because a new, very broad question accepts it, even though the fixed reference excluded a similar item as too insignificant.

1. **The fixed reference is not yet a fixed, pre-placement scoring population.** [coverage.json](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/coverage.json:37) distinguishes facts complete in the raw answer from facts already stored. For `a04-2`, v36b has a complete raw item but no stored item; v37 has both. The [design](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/prestart-experiment-design-2026-10-28.md:24) requires complete extraction and a passed quote check, but does not identify that pre-placement eligible set or show how to recover quote-check results independently of the old archive outcome. Using `fully_written` would exclude potential recoveries based on a later outcome.

2. **The original questions are not shown to be free of future knowledge.** Both arms use the original questions visible at each article’s historical run, while the seven added questions are fixed at July 1. The [expected homes](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/prestart-expected-homes-2026-10-28.json:92) include original IDs such as `v11`, `v13` and `v17`, but the reviewed files do not establish when those questions were created or what information they contain. The design therefore supports an incremental comparison against historical snapshots, but does not establish a common baseline known at the test start.

3. **The new homes introduce a broader acceptance standard without reconciling it with the fixed reference.** The new competition question asks about substitute products generally ([hypothesis](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/prestart-driver-hypothesis-2026-10-28.json:25)), while the [fixed reference](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/source-reference.json:13) excludes an Intel-versus-NVIDIA product comparison because it lacks quantified business-scale impact. The new questions and homes do not state how that original significance threshold applies. Consequently, the score may reward a broader definition of acceptable evidence rather than improved placement under the same standard.

4. **The score does not define when a fact has actually been retained.** The [design](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/prestart-experiment-design-2026-10-28.md:24) counts facts placed under an acceptable question, but some facts span several extracted records: for example, `a70-2` uses `e2` and `e3` in v36b ([coverage](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/coverage.json:685)). It does not say whether all parts and their meaning must survive, how duplicate placements count, or how the three control and experimental results determine an improvement. A higher count could therefore reflect partial or duplicate placement, or sampling variation, rather than retention of the fixed facts.
