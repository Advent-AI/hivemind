2. **The proposed stage scores can look good while the product loses content.** [The proposed evaluation](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/evaluation-and-trace-2026-10-28.md:28) scores placement only when extraction is complete and a correct existing page was shown. A system that omits difficult facts or never opens their pages can therefore improve that conditional score. It also allows several records to express one fact without requiring the complete meaning to reach the *same correct final page*. The current [audit calculation](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/audit.py:36) counts a fact as fully written when its listed records have stored IDs; it does not check their destinations. Acceptance needs a fixed source-fact denominator and a joint final result: complete meaning, correct source and correct destination, with misses and uncertain cases visible. Conditional stage scores are useful diagnostics alongside it.

3. **The reference’s source and meaning fields are not yet a reliable semantic answer key.** All 33 quoted passages occur verbatim in the stored source ledger, but matching text alone does not establish attribution or the full meaning. For example, [a71-3](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/source-reference.json:333) requires customer objections, while its quoted source passage only says the backup was scrapped; the objections appear in the next paragraph. The reference does not separately record the original speaker and uncertainty for each claim. Articles 70 and 71 also relay SemiAnalysis’s Kyber report, so their separate article counts must not be presented as independent corroboration. The review proposes these fields, but they have not yet been established and independently checked.

4. **The “no shown page” diagnosis misses a valid route.** Under [D211](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/DECISIONS.md:2680), extraction can link evidence to a question proposed by the same article, and [placement preserves those links](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/placement.py:63). The proposed table checks only whether an existing page was shown to placement. It must also follow a same-item candidate through its checks, matching and final writing before assigning a loss to a missing question. Otherwise it can blame question availability for a candidate or matching failure.

5. **The 49-case trace proves the empty answers, not the asserted economic destinations or all downstream facts independently.** The [trace script](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/trace-shown-misses-2026-10-28.py:17) starts with 49 previously labelled cases and checks those labels against requests; it does not establish that the labelled pages should take the statements. Its drop and final-write assertions read the derived ledger rather than independently replaying those stages. I checked the archived extraction answers: all 49 had empty original candidate links, consistent with the report. Article 52 e31 illustrates why destination review matters: a cloud provider’s borrowing cost and margin are not automatically evidence of NVIDIA’s guarantee amount. The report acknowledges this uncertainty; D212’s “49 have the destination shown” should not be read as 49 proven placement defects.

The new evaluation does not yet define how completeness, contradictory wording, attribution, split records and uncertain cases are judged consistently, or provide controls showing that those judgments cannot be gamed.
