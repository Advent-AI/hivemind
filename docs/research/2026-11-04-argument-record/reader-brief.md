# Reader brief: argument cards (H6, log.md step 2)

You did not write these cards, the prompt or the code. Read `cards-step2.md` beside you: for each question, its final argument card
(claims for and against the answer, each citing pieces of evidence or another question's conclusion), the full text of every piece the card
cites, and a one-line list of the evidence in force that the card does not cite.

Judge as a senior hedge-fund analyst who keeps one card per bet. For each question answer three things, each with the claim ids and evidence
ids it rests on:

1. **Supported.** Does every claim say only what its cited pieces say? List each claim that overstates, misstates or is not supported by its
   refs, with the words that go beyond the source.
2. **Nothing material left out.** Among the uncited evidence, is there a piece an analyst would hold on this card (a reported figure,
   guidance, a confirmed step or delay, a quantified estimate that moves the answer)? List each such piece with one sentence on why. A repeat
   of a fact the card already holds, background, or a statement that does not bear on the answer is not an omission.
3. **Relays not counted as independent.** Does any claim count several outlets carrying the same original report as independent sources, or
   mark as a relay a piece that is in fact a separate report? List each case.

Then give one overall line per question: sound / minor issues / material issue. Do not grade the rung; do not suggest prompt changes. Return
plain text, one section per question.
