**for**
- k1 [refs e84, e340, e386, e519; relays none; 4 independent] GPU deployment plans, Vera's $200 billion TAM, analyst commentary on 2027 orders, and $119 billion in unspecified-period supply commitments indicate opportunity but do not quantify FY2027 revenue.

- k3 [refs e15, e17, e336; relays none; 3 independent] Palantir is integrating NVIDIA Nemotron into sovereign-AI offerings, and AWS introduced serverless customization for Nemotron models; these adoption signals do not size NVIDIA revenue.

**against**
- k7 [refs e47, e366, e390, e391; relays none; 3 independent] Data-center project delays and reports of RTX 50-series holds, retail withdrawals, or production cuts are downside risks, but their FY2027 revenue effects are unquantified.
- k9 [refs e191, e195, e196; relays e195, e196; 1 independent] Bloomberg Intelligence reports Chinese infrastructure spending is often over budget and HBM supply bottlenecks may cap Chinese AI growth, creating a possible but unquantified NVIDIA demand constraint.
- k10 [refs e218, e410, e411, e335; relays none; 3 independent] AMD engagement and Broadcom's $10.8 billion AI-chip revenue and roughly $16 billion quarterly outlook signal competition, although NVIDIA's 97% server-GPU share remains high and FY2027 displacement is unquantified.
- k12 [refs e309, e461, e536, v8; relays none; 3 independent] Q2 guidance assumes zero China Data Center compute revenue and Q1 H20 shipments to China were zero; reported H200 approvals and the likely authorization partly offset, but do not quantify, this downside.
- k13 [refs e337, e353; relays none; 2 independent] Longer-horizon consensus sees about 32% annual revenue growth, and an analyst cautions that sustaining comparable growth is harder from the $215.9 billion FY2026 base; neither is a FY2027-specific forecast.
**would move it**: NVIDIA's next results and guidance, particularly whether sequential growth continues after Q2.; Disclosed orders, shipments, or guidance that could confirm or quantify FY2027 order visibility.; Confirmation and disclosed shipment or revenue impact of Chinese H200 authorizations.
**not known**: NVIDIA has supplied no FY2027 full-year guidance or revenue bridge.; The volume and revenue contribution of potential Chinese H200 sales are not quantified.; Order visibility, deployment plans, and monetization programs lack quantified FY2027 revenue contributions.
