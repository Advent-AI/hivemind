## Findings, most serious first

1. **A genuine change in a part can still be counted twice.** Old-1 `572` is a reasonable control for applying a premium only to the relevant share. Old-3 `5195` and `5369` add “roughly $4.1 billion for Q3 guidance above consensus” and then reuse “roughly $40 billion” of second-half compute upside. Q3 sits inside that second half. Old-3 `4101` explicitly avoids a separate Q3 addition “because it overlaps the second-half tracking uplift.” The $396B annual estimate predates the earnings release, so updating the quarters is reasonable. The unanswered question is how much of the older compute premium remains after those updates. The full $40B addition decides the rung in `5195` and `5369`. The amount that overlaps cannot be recovered from the answers. Similarly, old-2 `1788` says the Blackwell/Vera Rubin forecast “supports further growth,” but gives no separate amount from that forecast. The census should distinguish these uncertain cases from clear additions. A search for component names cannot establish the full count.
