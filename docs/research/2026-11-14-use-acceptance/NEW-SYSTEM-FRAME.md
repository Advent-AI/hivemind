## 2. The first blocker: worthwhile questions the step declines (gate 1, the count by written choices)

The bounded question the council set: **can a general, permitted change recover a worthwhile question the system declines,
without making it answer questions it should refuse?** Answered from the stored records alone
(`clean-arch/e2e-comparison-2026-11-13/reachability/RESULT.md` with its theme census; `CHOICES-FOR-ALEX-2026-11-13.md`; the status
page's record of D467 to D482).

**The population.** In July the step's first pass declined eleven of the 40 topics. Two blind readers agree on ten: six are
rightly declined (five matters; four of the six are topics the first step should not have opened: a completed stake, a stake
too small to matter, a background sentence, an outcome no public material settles; two are the survey question Alex ruled out,
D417); four are worth a question the article supports, on two matters. One is split. Reaching the scaled 26 needs all four and
no loss elsewhere.

| Lead | The mechanism on record | A general change? | What stands against it |
|---|---|---|---|
| the memory-price pair | the first step's shape of the topic: a rolling twelve months; a second figure (a bank's estimate of one rack's memory cost) that no public material settles | none on record that is permitted: two first-step candidates aimed at this class failed their lines (c15yc, c17), first-step sentence changes on these articles are stopped (D473, D477), and the answer-choice step declines a rolling window by Alex's own ruling (D417) | the stops themselves |
| the rental-price pair (one article) | the step's rule "a matter that no theme in the request's themes covers", applied to twelve themes shown by id and name only. The census: the same topic declined in 5 of 9 attempts and given choices in 4 (three under "Semiconductor cycle", one under another theme); memory prices go under "Semiconductor cycle" in every attempt, though that theme's stored watch list holds only industry revenue growth. Six topics in all were declined for coverage in 12 attempts, three of them given choices in other attempts | in principle: tell the step what each theme carries, so that coverage is decided against a definition and not a name | what a theme carries is a product definition in `poc/universe.json`, which the old system shares; its stored definition of that theme does not include prices, so showing it as it is would make the step decline prices more consistently, not less; the definition itself is the first of the three choices written for Alex on 2026-11-13, untested. D477 stopped a theme or ladder change for this boundary after the check that the question, where it was opened, was decisive in no judgement of that window. The Stage A readers had ruled these refusals right on the list as it stands |

**Upside and protection.** The rental-price pair is at most 2 of the 4 the scaled count needs (22 to 24 of 26). None of the six
right refusals is a refusal for theme coverage, so a change of what the themes carry would not by itself turn them into
questions; the three other topics declined for coverage (a lidar ban, the CPU-to-GPU ratio in inference, Huawei's chip output)
have never been read for whether they are worth a question.

**Answer: no candidate.** The records establish a mechanism for one of the two matters, and for that one the change is a
product definition already put to Alex and stopped by a council decision, worth at most half the gap, with nothing on record
that the recovered question would receive sound choices or matter to a judgement. For the other matter no permitted change
exists. On the stored evidence the count by written choices is not reachable by a permitted change to the answer-choice step:
its distance is set upstream, by the topics the first step opens (rightly refused ones, and the memory shapes).
