## 1. A rule the new week can affect (design sections 5 and 6)

The new week's eligible topics are judged apart: too few below 6 topics; clean = no agreed fault on the four zero-fault lines
in any attempt, consistent direction, nine in ten due days, ceil(35n/44) and ceil(28n/44); the fresh week is shown beside it
and pooled only as description. Section 6 predeclares what each outcome changes:

- **Clean and no topic declined:** with the fresh week, no refusal in at least 13 topics; at July's rate (11 of 40) that
  happens about 3 times in 200. The council then takes up the design of a reading sized as the first gate's acceptance, and
  no repair of the answer-choice step is opened meanwhile.
- **A condition of the topic left out of the choices, on another matter:** D545's own trigger is met (the same mechanism on
  two distinct questions) and a repair design opens at $0.
- **A worthwhile topic declined, or two or more right refusals of topics the first step should not have opened:** July's
  pattern holds on such news; with "no candidate" at this step, the first step's topics are the blocker and the council takes
  up whether D473 and D477 are reopened.
- **Another fault on one or two attempts:** one case; recorded with its trace and the model's account; no design from it.
- **Too few topics, the two counts apart, or an open mark:** nothing concluded; no further week automatically.
