## 2. What decides readiness: the product comparison (D663's design, still to be written)

- **Material:** the six unread weeks 2026-07-20 to 08-24.
  - **New system:** run fresh from a library it built over 2026-07-01 to 07-19.
  - **Old system:** its stored output for the same weeks (the sealed run, D206).
- **Cards:** every question each system holds at each week's end, with its last answer, plus each week's big matters, census-listed by both
  families from the articles before any system output is read. Planted wrong answers and repeats go in every batch.
- **Reader:** the council (D663), with splits left as "cannot tell", and Alex spot-checking a fixed sample of splits.
- **Measures** (proposed; the council to set the numbers):
  - **C:** big matters covered by a question that is worth tracking;
  - **A:** answers agreed supported, among questions agreed worth tracking;
  - **N:** noise, the questions agreed not worth tracking.
- **Rule**, written before the run:
  - **Better:** C at least as high, and A higher by at least a margin the size supports.
  - **No worse:** C within 1 matter a week on average, and A within a margin.
  - **Worse:** otherwise.
  - The planted controls must be caught, or the reading is void.
- **Size:** six weeks gives about 42 new and 90 old questions, enough to see a difference of about 20 points in A, which is to be computed in
  the design. C is counted per week, so six weeks gives six paired points.
