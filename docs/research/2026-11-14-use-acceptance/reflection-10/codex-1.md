I propose **10 percentage points as the material difference**, roughly one additional usable answer per ten important matters. Use a prewritten 95% interval for the new-minus-old difference, accounting for matters and weeks shared by both systems:

- **Better:** the lower bound is above **+10 points**.
- **No worse shown:** the lower bound is above **−10 points** and the better rule is unmet. This can include a possible improvement that remains unproven.
- **Worse:** the upper bound is below **−10 points**.
- **Unclear:** everything else. A missed control or a comparison that fails its input checks yields **no verdict**.
