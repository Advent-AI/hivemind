**What the last stretch taught (process, not product):**
1. My rounds have been spent proving infrastructure (live store, split writer, probe harness) to a high standard, while the gates have
   not moved. Each candidate was small (two topics, a few sends), and none was aimed at a gate's largest loss.
2. Gate 1's misses are zero-fault lines on 131 sends judged by model readers. Six faults of six different meanings are not a class. A
   prompt arm aimed at any one of them is case-fitting (AGENTS §2), and the 44 may not be tuned on anyway (D426).
3. So gate 1 cannot be passed by fixing "the six"; only a step that is right more often everywhere can pass it. That needs
   development material outside the 44 and one measurement on the 44.
