# A paired gate-3 comparison on the same matters: feasibility at $0 (D627), 2026-11-16

**Answer: pairing does not rescue the comparison.** It closes by the council's condition: "the material, comparability or sensitivity
cannot support a decision within the ceiling."

**The material.** The only weeks no new-system run has read are 2026-07-20 to 08-24 (`comparison-feasibility/RESULT.md`, D613). The
old system's served three-month run opened 11, 17, 2, 2, 9 and 8 questions in those six weeks: 49 in all. The new system, from a
carried library, opens about 5 to 7 a week (the carried fortnight: 13 topics in two weeks). Matched matters, the same bet asked by
both, are expected at about 15 to 20 over the six weeks, given how often the side-by-side found different shapes for the same matter.

**The power** (`paired_power.py` → `power-output.txt`; exact two-sided McNemar at 5%, 80% power), the difference in supported answers
needed:

| Matched pairs | 30% of pairs discordant | 50% discordant |
|---:|---|---|
| 15 | not reachable | 49 points |
| 20 | not reachable | 44 points |
| 30 | 28 points | 37 points |
| 49 (every old question matched, an upper bound) | 22 points | 29 points |

The unpaired design needed about 31 points (`comparison/COMPARISON-SIZING.md`). At the realistic 15 to 20 pairs, pairing needs more,
not less.

**Cost, not reached:** the new system's six weeks would cost an estimated $3 to $5 of the about $9.3 left, before any reading.

**So:** a paired gate-3 comparison cannot decide anything that matters within what is left. This does not show the systems equal.
