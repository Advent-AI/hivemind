## The pattern I see, to be tested by readers who do not know it

The four stable matters are ones where the news hands over one thing with its date or its yearly report: a shipment the
company dates, a regulator's decision, a figure in the annual report, a calendar-year total with a stated plan. Every unstable
one is a matter where someone has to choose the yardstick: for a market price, what is measured, over which period and under
which theme; for an event with no stated date, the deadline; for a matter with several actors or parts, which one; and
whether a small matter is worth a question at all. By my own sorting: given, 4 of 4 the same; chosen, 0 of 7.

If readers sorting the topics by "stated or chosen" without seeing the judged questions do not reproduce that split, the
pattern is mine and not the record's.
