## Where I think your statement is too narrow

You name the matter with several separately meaningful parts. On the record that class is small: six topics declined three
times of three, two of them worthwhile matters by the readers' own verdicts, in 104 stored topics. If that were the core
problem, one product choice about two-figure topics would bring the system most of the way. I do not think the record says
that. Facts you did not weigh, each checkable:

1. **The same news gives different questions from run to run.** Open the three July runs' topics
   (`poc/data/clean-e2e-full-2026-11-13/c1/cases/cases.json`, `c2`, `c3`): the same fourteen days opened 15, 10 and 15 topics. The
   cloud companies' 2026 spending is a figure in c1 (topic 6), a figure with a threshold in c2 (topic 4) and a yes-or-no in c3
   (topic 5); the rental price a yearly average in c1 and "will it keep falling" in c2 and c3; memory prices a rolling twelve
   months (c1), two things at once (c2), two yes-or-no questions (c3); the Intel stake opened in c1 and c3 and not in c2. None
   of these is the multi-part class, and each lands in a different place downstream.
2. **One topic in five never gets a question it can be judged on, for several reasons, not one.** In the map's data
   (`second-week/proofs/first-gate-map.json`) 21 of 104 topics were declined in at least two of three attempts (9 of 44, 9 of 40,
   0 of 7, 3 of 13): no main part, a period that is not a year, no listed theme, a quarter, and topics the readers say should
   not have been opened. What they share is that the first step wrote something the next step could only take or leave.
3. **On the reference's questions the losses were at this joint in both systems** (D445, D454), and when the questions were
   given the new path agreed on 6.33 of 7 against 4.0 when it opened its own (D466; one comparison, seven questions). The
   opening review (`clean-arch/e2e-comparison-2026-11-13/opening-review/OPENING-REVIEW.md`) then found that of 71 lost
   question-draws only 14 are shown breaches of the standard; 27 are choices the standard does not settle and 19 unresolved.
4. **A dozen wording changes at the two steps since 2026-11-13, none adopted, several moving the error** (D441, D465, D473,
   D479, D491), with D483's own diagnosis: "one call must decide what to ask, its scope, its years and where facts go, so local
   rules interact."
5. **Every run starts from an empty library**, so every run measures the invention of the whole list of questions again.
   D466 put the choice of a real prior library or an empty start to Alex; I find no answer of his on it.

So my statement: **the system has no owner and no tight definition for the decision "what exactly is the question"; it is
made in pieces, from one article's wording, anew in every run, and the standard leaves the big choices of shape open.** The
multi-part topic is one way that shows; the unstable shape, the unanswerable period, the unworthy topic and the unsteady
readers are others.
