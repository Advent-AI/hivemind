**Hivemind’s goal is to give an analyst a continuing, source-backed research record that helps answer important investment questions. The project has several quantified checks, but no single agreed number that proves that whole job is ready for production.**

For example, an analyst tracking capital spending should be able to open the question, see the company’s forecast alongside other relevant evidence, understand why the current answer was chosen, and follow that answer as new information arrives. A test can show that a passage was saved or that an answer choice is well formed without showing that the analyst received the right facts at the right time.

## 1. What the analyst uses it for

The clean design says the system should maintain “a dossier of the evidence and events behind each thesis” and keep the connections clear enough that “a reader can understand why a judgement exists.” It distinguishes the question, the evidence, and the judgement. ([clean architecture PRD, §1](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/PRD.md:5))

The production design adds a lifecycle: questions have answer choices and a due date; incoming information updates their assessment; the question and its reasons remain available when it closes. A closed question can inform a related question. ([system PRD, Purpose and Principles](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/00-system.md:9); [closing PRD](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/prd/07-final-verdict.md:8))

**My inference:** the useful product is a maintained research workspace, not merely a list of extracted facts. It should help an analyst decide what matters, inspect the supporting and opposing sources, and understand what has changed.

## 6. My answers to Alex’s three questions

**What are we trying to achieve?** A dependable, evolving research record that lets an analyst see important questions, the evidence behind them, and a defensible current answer. I would revise that description if Alex intends the product to make a narrower decision or to replace a specific part of an analyst’s workflow rather than support the whole record. ([clean PRD §1](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/clean-arch/PRD.md:5))

**Is measurement part of the problem?** Yes: the current checks provide useful evidence but do not yet establish the complete user outcome, and empty-start results answer a different question from behavior with an established library. The second-week review also exposed reader disagreement, including conflicting instructions about a due date; this is evidence to resolve the measurement procedure, not grounds to erase the failed gate. ([second-week result](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/research/2026-11-14-use-acceptance/second-week/RESULT.md:117); [D559](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/DECISIONS.md:903))
