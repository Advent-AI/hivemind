## What the answers show beyond the lines

- **The Vera Rubin question: two possible causes, not separated (council round 16).** In c20's sends the reasoning weighs the product
  question and gives the missing shipment date as the reason not to open it ("We don't open due to no date"; "no separate thesis because
  the event has already occurred and no date for shipments"), and it files the statement under the new revenue question ("If we don't
  create, link to revenue"; "Link Vera Rubin to new revenue thesis? It informs future revenue path … yes"). The adopted request's one send
  that did not open it gives the same date reason ("no product_node thesis possible without a date"); its other two set the fiscal
  year-end as the check date and opened it. Three sends an arm cannot tell whether the revenue question made "do not open" easier or the
  arms simply fell on different sides of the same doubt. Where the Vera Rubin question is already open (later articles), c20 links such
  statements to it as c8 does, sometimes to the revenue question as well.
- **The revenue question takes in more than the company's figures.** Of the 97 statements c20 linked to a new revenue or margin question, 66
  carry a company figure and 31 do not: the supply bottleneck, Vera Rubin production, and, on one article, five statements about AMD in two
  of three sends, filed as "competitive evidence" under NVIDIA's fiscal 2027 revenue (c8 leaves them without a question). This is the
  pattern of production's revenue question, which held 126 statements in the fresh week.
- **Statements with a question.** Over the same 30 sends, statements with no question fall from 118 (c8) to 45 (c20).
- **Shape follows the article.** A level where the article states a level (the consensus in dollars), mostly a growth rate where it states
  the 70% outlook: over a week this can give one fiscal year two shapes, which step two was written to test and did not run.
- **Not asked for, in scope:** the fiscal 2027 gross margin, opened on three articles (in 6 of 9 sends there).
- **Supply commitments** opened by c8 in only 1 of 3 sends on the article where the stored draw opened it: the adopted request's own spread.

## What it means

- The hypothesis held on what it predicted (the questions open once the rule allows them, without number cards) and met a stop condition
  written beforehand: on the one judged case a product question was not opened. The line stops by that condition; the cause is a lead.
- The new questions do change where news goes: product and competitor statements are filed under revenue. Whether each such link is right
  is a question of filing quality the probe did not judge (the standard lets product, demand and competitor news inform a revenue question
  when it bears on it); it is the pattern D382 was wary of, not a proof of it.
- Not shown: that a revenue question must swallow a product question; that statements without a company figure should never be linked to
  revenue; that either system is better; that the kept figures are usable today.
- **Where the kept figures stand today (counted at $0 on the week's clean draw):** 567 statements reach the judge's store and 382 of them
  have no question; the 70% outlook and the $406 billion and $412 billion estimates appear in none of the 37 judgement requests; when a
  question opened, the later-linking step judged 516 earlier statements and linked 2. The use Alex approved on 2026-11-08 for a kept figure
  ("it becomes evidence when a related view opens and the reference when views are judged", `LOG.md` §17) is not happening for them.
