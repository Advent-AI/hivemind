### 3. The next breakthrough angle

I do **not** see a bounded change on record ready to run as a fix. The best next angle is a **$0, source-to-answer trace of financial-figure delivery in a continuing library**: take the known important figures, identify each related question that actually exists, and follow the fact through its saved record, links and exact final judging request. Include facts filed under a different question, not just unlinked facts, and read the source passages.

- **Falsifier:** if the apparently missing figures either do not bear on any existing question, already reach its judging request, or have different causes that no one intervention covers, this is not a supported common delivery candidate.
- **What would move it:** a substantial, counted class of source-backed facts that should inform existing questions but disappear through the same identifiable step, plus a stored-data proof that a proposed change delivers them without pulling in unrelated facts.
- **What closes it:** the falsifier above, or a proposed change that cannot deliver the decisive cases or that introduces comparable wrong links. Finding a missing fact alone does not prove an answer changes; that needs a separately justified test.
- **Spend:** **$0 for the trace.** No paid send is supported yet. A later bounded test would need a reviewed design, controls and its own ceiling under D439; the user’s approximately $10 remaining is an upper constraint, not a target.

This respects D505’s choice to use figures as evidence for related questions. It also tests an actual delivery defect rather than reopening the rejected proposal to create financial questions. The fortnight’s figure census is a lead, but its fixed selector, partly shown groups and reader controls leave the delivery question open. [Fortnight result, reading 4](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/research/2026-11-14-use-acceptance/carried-run/RESULT.md), [main-numbers probe](/Users/lchingen/hivemind/docs/research/2026-11-14-use-acceptance/main-numbers/RESULT.md).
