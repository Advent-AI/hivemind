THE AUTHOR'S CLAIMS TO ATTACK (say for each whether it is right, wrong or incomplete, with evidence you read)
1. "One dial": the sentences the gold keeps and the sentences it rejects, among other parties' actions/figures/judgements with a name but no amount, cannot be told apart by any sentence of the request or any code rule over the model's labels; the gold splits them by the reader's judgement of tense/context ("has chosen" kept vs "is establishing" not kept).
2. Since D264 (extraction files statements itself) and D209 (a statement with no home is dropped), the takes line has become the keep gate; every figure question's takes line ends with the same tail "and what makes that figure more or less likely: the demand, supply, competition and pricing behind it", which the model cites as the reason to keep (probe-v75-residue.txt) and which makes v2 the catch-all; "narrowest question" cannot narrow when every tail is identical.
3. The reject rows of role-v74 and review-v74 carry exceptions ("unless…", "it does not cover…") broader than the rows; the model cites the exception and keeps (probes); the 2026-10-31 record (docs/research/2026-10-30-noise/SEALED-2026-10-31.md) found each exception read as "always keep"; v77 (exceptions first) raised recall and noise one for one.
4. The "is it new / already known" test (standard line: what an analyst following the company knew before the item) cannot be answered by a reader with no memory of the company; the model answers "new" for any assertive sentence with figures; blind human readers agreed with the gold 93-95% on clear cases and 70% on these borderline items.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR (beyond the four claims)
- Contradictions between role, closing check, category block, takes lines and schema descriptions that decide keep/reject/link in opposite directions for the same shape of sentence.
- Instructions that force the noise: e.g. "a page with eight such statements yields eight evidence items, not a selection"; "even when it gives no period, basis or exact amount, because the weight is judged later"; the category block's "read:" lines (T6: "partnerships, investments"; T8: "the facts and figures the author cites").
- Definitions that are missing or abstract (no closed set, no example) for the residual classes: an arrangement in place vs a new step; common knowledge with a figure; a named party's judgement that states a level/direction/date for a question's quantity vs a characterisation; another company's matter "with a path".
- Design-level causes: the one call doing four jobs (evidence, links, candidates, groups/identity); the drop of unfiled statements; the quote length (a quote spanning a kept and a not-kept sentence counts as both: 13 of v94's 33 noise on the mixed set); the window starting with an empty ledger (the system is "cold" while the gold's reader is "warm").
- Whether the standard itself gives a resolvable reading for the borderline shape that the prompt does not implement (e.g. 5.1 kind 2 "with a size, date or name" + D250 item 1 "no new step" = a named party's NEW action; D240 lower weight vs D243 cautious).
