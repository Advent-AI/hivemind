The proposal is not ready as written. Removing the fallback restores the standard’s requirement to find a real home, but the new “same money” sentence turns some useful financial connections into overly broad claims of equivalence.

For example, an equipment maker’s orders can inform customers’ future investment. They do not automatically measure those customers’ total capital spending, or spending in a particular year.

1. Clause-by-clause comparison

All proposed quotations below come from definition_no_fallback in links-rules.json. Its stored counterpart exactly matches the current constant in placement.py.

1.1 “decided after you have decided it is evidence”

Verdict: consistent.

The standard says: “分析员会记 → 留为证据”, followed by filing under the narrowest open question. Section 5.1 also says: “问题不限于库里已有的”.

This correctly separates two decisions: whether the statement is useful evidence, and whether an available question can receive it. A missing home does not retroactively make the statement meaningless; D209 nevertheless requires dropping it from the product.

1.2 “the id of the narrowest open question … whose answer this statement makes more or less likely”

Verdict: consistent.

Section 5.1: “因为它让某个问题的答案更可能或更不可能”.

The filing instruction: “挂到最窄的那个已开问题”.

D265 confirms “keeps the narrower one only”. The proposal preserves both relevance and the narrowest-question requirement.

1.3 “as that question’s own figure, step or estimate or through the demand, supply, competition or pricing behind its answer”

Verdict: consistent with §§5.1 and 5.3; there is a separate historical tension concerning company-wide questions.

Section 5.3 requires being able to explain “它通过需求、供给、竞争、定价这类路径动了在研公司的哪个问题”.

Section 5.1 includes company figures, third-party actions and figures, estimates, and policy steps. Therefore, the statement need not directly report the quantity being asked about. A defensible indirect connection is sufficient.

However, D207 states: “company-wide figure questions take only their own figures, guidance and management statements.” The signed standard’s B7 says “以 D207 为准”. The proposal retains wording broader than that older restriction. Later decisions D273 and D274 explicitly introduce and freeze the broader behavior. Removing the fallback does not resolve that documentary tension.

1.4 “a question’s quantity can be read from either side of the same money”

Verdict: consistent as a limited explanation of a genuine transaction; has no source as a general equivalence rule.

Section 5.1 permits third-party figures, and §5.3 permits reasoning through economic relationships. Those provisions already allow an analyst to connect a seller’s sales with its customers’ purchases.

But the standard’s test is whether evidence changes an answer’s likelihood. It does not say that related financial quantities are interchangeable. “Can inform the answer” follows from the standard; “measures the quantity” requires a further factual justification.

D273 supports supplying the connection through analysis: “the analyst supplies the connection”. It does not establish every connection asserted in the new sentence.

1.5 “what sellers book from a set of buyers measures those buyers’ spending”

Verdict: consistent for the corresponding purchases from those sellers; keeps wider if treated as sufficient for the buyers’ broader spending question.

The standard permits “同一总量的另一种人群或代用口径” and explicitly describes “another population or proxy of one aggregate”. Therefore, imperfect coverage is not itself grounds for rejecting a useful connection.

But that permission concerns evidence about the same aggregate. It does not make a seller’s total revenue equal to one customer group’s spending.

A wholesaler selling to both hospitals and pharmacies could grow entirely through hospital sales. Its total growth does not, by itself, establish growing pharmacy purchases. The proposed wording can encourage that mistake when combined with the retained “another … population” clause.

This does not justify requiring an exact customer breakdown before filing anything. An aggregate can still be useful evidence when the analyst can explain its connection to the particular question.

1.6 “buyers’ orders, backlogs and budgets measure their sellers’ coming sales”

Verdict: keeps wider; the blanket measurement claim has no source.

Section 5.1 permits customer actions and published figures. Section 6 includes “客户公布的订单积压” as supporting evidence. Neither says that these quantities measure suppliers’ future sales.

The three quantities differ:

- Orders placed with a supplier can inform that supplier’s future sales.
- A customer’s reported backlog may contain orders received from its own customers. That is evidence about its demand, not the same transaction as its purchases from an upstream supplier.
- A budget is planned expenditure. It need not become orders, and need not go to any particular seller.

Orders and backlogs also need not convert into revenue at their stated value or timing. For example, Babcock & Wilcox’s filing explicitly describes backlog cancellation and adjustment risks. Revenue recognition itself depends on satisfying the relevant performance obligations. [Babcock & Wilcox filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1630805/000163080526000018/bw-20251231.htm); [IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/).

The standard supports using these as evidence. It does not support grouping them as interchangeable measurements of “the same money”.

1.7 “compare the specific questions before a company-wide revenue or margin question and take that one only, never a broader question beside it”

Verdict: consistent.

The filing instruction says “最窄的那个已开问题”. D265 describes removing the broader link while keeping the narrower one.

Nothing in the new financial explanation requires a second, broader filing. Recognizing a connection from another side of a transaction is compatible with selecting the narrowest relevant question.

1.8 “a shared company, industry, event or ecosystem is not a connection”

Verdict: consistent when read as “sharing these alone is insufficient”.

Section 5.3 rejects statements connected “只因同一篇文章、同一场活动或同一个生态挨着、说不出这条路径”.

The important limitation is “只因”: an actual demand or supply relationship still qualifies. This clause should continue to constrain the new examples; belonging to the same supply chain cannot itself prove the proposed financial equivalence.

1.9 “the statement need not repeat the question’s words (a variant, sibling or other name of the subject counts)”

Verdict: consistent for another expression or variant of the same underlying matter; keeps wider if “sibling” means any related subject.

D209 identifies failure to recognize “the stopgap rack NVL72x2 … as the Kyber/Rubin Ultra matter”. The standard similarly says “同一代产品的几个里程碑” can supply evidence to one question.

These support recognizing the same matter despite different names. They do not support automatically treating a separate sibling product or business as the same subject. The opening relevance test remains necessary.

1.10 “whoever says it, and even when it gives no period, basis or exact amount, because the weight is judged later”

Verdict: consistent as a prohibition on automatic rejection, not as permission to invent a connection.

Section 5.2 says “有名有姓的动作缺金额、缺日期也是证据，分量可以低一点”. D264 describes filing “whoever says it, with or without a period or amount”.

“Basis” is not expressly covered by those quoted permissions. It is acceptable if it means a missing accounting or measurement basis does not automatically disqualify useful evidence. It would contradict §5.3 if interpreted as allowing filing without any explainable economic connection.

1.11 “an estimate for another period or population of a question’s quantity is filed under it when no open question covers that period”

Verdict: consistent for relevant alternate-period evidence and alternate measures of the same aggregate; keeps wider if read as an unconditional population substitution.

The standard says: “按日历年的估计是财年那题的证据，两种年份差得多时分量低一点”.

It also permits “another population or proxy of one aggregate”. D207 records that “aggregates admit other populations”.

Those passages support flexibility. They do not make every population reporting a similarly named quantity relevant to every other population. The proposal’s “a question’s quantity” must retain that distinction. The new “same money” sentence makes this existing ambiguity more consequential.

1.12 “plus the ids of this answer’s candidates the statement supports”

Verdict: consistent.

D264 expressly includes “plus the answer’s own candidates” and says evidence linked only to candidates follows them. This is a documented qualification to the open-question wording, not an invented escape from “no home”.

1.13 “empty when no question and no candidate is connected, however important it sounds”

Verdict: consistent.

The standard says: “没有问题接，记 no home 扔掉，评估时逐条读”.

D209 requires unfiled evidence to go to the dropped record. The links description correctly specifies an empty result; it need not repeat the subsequent dropping and evaluation procedure.

2. Removing the fallback

The removed sentence is:

“when no specific question is connected, a statement that moves the demand, supply, competition or pricing behind the company’s results goes to the company-wide revenue question of its period”.

Verdict: removing the automatic destination brings the rule closer to the standard.

Neither §5.1’s broad definition of useful evidence nor §6’s list of important business questions gives total revenue the job of receiving everything without a specific home. Something can affect costs, financing or competitive position without adequately informing the revenue question.

There is, however, explicit decision history behind the sentence. D273 introduced “the company-wide revenue question as the home of indirect evidence”; D274 froze that exact rule. It would therefore be inaccurate to call the existing sentence source-less.

The precise conclusion is: the fallback has a recorded implementation decision behind it, but creates tension with the standard’s relevance and no-home requirements. Removing it does not prohibit genuinely relevant indirect evidence from reaching a revenue question under the opening test.

D355 also records that “removing the total-revenue fallback alone leaves good indirect evidence unlinked”. That is evidence of a remaining filing problem, not proof that the standard requires the fallback.

3. The three examples

“a drug wholesaler’s sales to pharmacies measure pharmacy purchases”

Conditionally correct. These are opposite sides of the corresponding drug purchases from that wholesaler. They do not measure all pharmacy purchases, all pharmacy expenditure, or necessarily cash paid during the same period. The example is useful if that limited meaning remains clear.

“a lender’s new loans to small firms measure small-firm borrowing”

Conditionally correct. New loans actually advanced measure gross borrowing from that lender. They do not measure borrowing from all lenders, net borrowing after repayments, or spending by those firms. Loan approvals or unused facilities would also not be equivalent to amounts borrowed.

“an equipment maker’s order book measures its customers’ coming capital spending”

Too broad as written. It can inform future equipment investment. It does not necessarily measure total capital spending, spending in a particular period, or purchases ultimately completed. The maker’s order book may also contain services or equipment bought for resale. The defensible connection is evidence about future investment, not an unconditional equivalence.

All three examples are general in wording: no chip terminology, NVIDIA, named covered company, or company-specific number appears. They span distribution, lending and equipment manufacturing.

I cannot certify “none taken from a test article” from the supplied proposal and diagnosis: neither provides their provenance. I found no basis to allege copying, but generic wording alone does not establish independent origin.

4. Reverse check and remaining conflicts

Reading from the standard back to the proposal:

- “问题不限于库里已有的” is preserved by deciding evidence status first and allowing candidate links.
- Third-party figures and indirect economic relationships remain eligible. The new sentence is not needed to authorize them; its legitimate purpose would be to explain how to recognize them.
- “最窄的那个已开问题” remains explicit.
- “没有问题接 … 扔掉” is restored without an automatic revenue destination.
- The standard’s requirement to explain which question is affected remains in the opening clause. The unconditional “measure” examples risk weakening it.
- The standard’s flexibility about periods and populations remains. That flexibility must not become an assertion that different populations or financial quantities are identical.

There is no inherent contradiction between the retained narrowest-question rule and recognizing a genuine transaction from either side. The material problems are the broad measurement claims, their interaction with “another … population”, and the unresolved D207 restriction on company-wide questions.

The diagnosis does provide a written rationale—“模型缺这一层定义”—so the growth is not unexplained.
