1. **Source for each rule in the three descriptions**

   - **`speaker`:** “Who says it in this item: the outlet, the author or the person quoted” ([extraction.py:45](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/extraction.py:45)). Recording a speaker follows the [standard’s evidence-record rule](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:237). The closed list “the outlet, the author or the person quoted” is **unsourced** in the cited standard and decisions.
   - **`original_who`:** “The party whose statement, figure or action it is” ([extraction.py:46](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/extraction.py:46)) partly follows “原始判断者” and D220’s “Original claimant” requirement ([standard §5.4](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:378); [D220](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/DECISIONS.md:2764)). Treating the subject of an action or figure as its original claimant is **unsourced**. The examples “the insurer” and “the regulator,” even when reported “without attribution,” apply that unsourced extension.
   - **`original_who`:** “the outlet or author only for its own estimate, analysis or unnamed-source report” ([extraction.py:47](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/extraction.py:47)) has partial support for an author’s own estimate or analysis in “原始判断者” ([standard §5.4](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:378)). The exhaustive “only” restriction and assigning an unnamed-source report to the outlet or author as the original party are **unsourced**.
   - **`original_day`:** “The day that party made it, as an ISO day when the item gives one” ([extraction.py:49](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/extraction.py:49)) follows the original statement-day requirement in [standard §5.4](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:385) and the ISO-day storage rule in [D203](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/DECISIONS.md:2604). The example “signed on 3 March 2026”: `2026-03-03` treats an action’s date as the statement day; that inference is **unsourced**.
   - **`original_day`:** “empty when the item gives none or only a month” follows the unknown-day rule in [standard §5.4](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:385) and D203’s “keeps an ISO day and blanks anything else.”
   - **`original_day`:** “the publication day only when the item is that party’s own release, filing, call or analysis” ([extraction.py:50](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/extraction.py:50)) is **unsourced** as a categorical restriction or exception. The standard says an *unknown original statement day* must not be filled with the reporting day; it does not make document type the test.

2. **Contradictions**

   - **Action date versus statement date:** “signed on 3 March 2026”: `2026-03-03` ([extraction.py:49](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/extraction.py:49)) can assign the signing date even when the date of the statement reporting it is unknown. The standard says “原始发言日不知道就写不知道” ([§5.4](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:385)); [role-v53:17](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/prompts/role-v53.txt:17) asks for the “statement day.”
   - **A known statement day equal to publication day:** “the publication day only when the item is that party’s own release, filing, call or analysis” conflicts with “as an ISO day when the item gives one” in the same description. For example, a news article can explicitly report that a named executive spoke on the article’s publication day. The standard prohibits filling an **unknown** statement day with the reporting day; it does not prohibit recording a known one.
   - **Actor versus original claimant:** “the regulator fined the bank, a newspaper reported”: “the regulator” ([extraction.py:47](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/extraction.py:47)) makes the actor the original party although the example does not say the regulator was the original source of the report. That conflicts with preserving “原始判断者” ([standard §5.4](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:378)) and with T7’s “original source” and T8’s “original attribution” ([categories-v53:29](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/prompts/categories-v53.json:29); [categories-v53:33](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/prompts/categories-v53.json:33)).
   - **Tier instructions:** No direct contradiction found. The descriptions assign attribution fields; [role-v53:17](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/prompts/role-v53.txt:17) and T7/T8 assign source tiers. The actor-versus-source problem can nevertheless make the attribution misleading even if the tier is correctly assigned.

3. **Wording fitted to one company, industry or bench article:** None identified in the text. The insurer, broker, regulator and bank examples are generic; the text alone does not establish whether an example was derived from a bench article.

4. **Cases where following a description mislabels or removes something the standard keeps**

   - The signing-date example can store an event date as a statement date, even when the latter is unknown. The evidence need not be removed for its record to be wrong.
   - The regulator example can label the subject of a reported action as its original source without evidence that the regulator made the statement.
   - The publication-day restriction can leave a known original statement day empty when a news article explicitly gives that same day.

   No instruction in the three descriptions directly removes an evidence item.

5. **Ambiguities**

   - “The party whose statement, figure or action it is” does not say whether a figure’s subject, the party that produced it, or the party that first reported it is `original_who` when those differ.
   - “Who says it in this item: the outlet, the author or the person quoted” does not resolve which to use when an author reports a quoted person, or when a company or agency speaks as an organisation.
   - “The day that party made it” does not distinguish the day of an action from the day someone stated or reported it; its signing example points toward the former, while the role asks for the latter.
   - “the publication day only when” does not say whether an own release’s publication day is sufficient when that release repeats an earlier statement, or whether the original statement day must independently be known.
