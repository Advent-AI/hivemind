The relevant category instruction is: “Officially or company-confirmed events are facts; unnamed-source claims and unconfirmed reports are rumours; quoted company or official statements are party statements; the journalist's own judgements are third-party views.”

I chose party_statement because e4 says the arrangement was “confirmed” by three executives with business ties to NVIDIA. I treated their confirmation as a statement by involved parties, not an official or company-confirmed fact.

I considered **fact**, but rejected it because the item does not identify these executives as speaking officially for NVIDIA or the participating cloud providers. I considered **rumour**, but rejected it because the report says the executives confirmed the arrangement, rather than presenting it as unconfirmed or attributed to unnamed sources. I rejected **third_party_view** because this is a reported confirmation, not the journalist’s judgement.
