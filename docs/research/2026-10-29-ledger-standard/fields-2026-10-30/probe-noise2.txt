The relevant rule was: “a partner's or customer's adoption or integration, and a change to the plan, configuration or timing of the product that carries the company's value, are not product details.”

I treated Sharon AI’s “strategic collaboration with NVIDIA” as a partner action bearing on NVIDIA’s products. But the quote only calls the collaboration “a pivotal moment” and describes Sharon AI’s mission; it gives no concrete adoption, integration, or change to a product plan, configuration, or timing. The reject-list line therefore should have applied, and e2 should not have been kept.
