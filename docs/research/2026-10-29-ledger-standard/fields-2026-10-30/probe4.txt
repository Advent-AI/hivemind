The wording that led me to choose `third_party_view` was:

- System: “Use only this item's information. Report plans as plans; do not infer committed funding, orders, deliveries or revenue.”
- System: “Identify the speaker and, for a retelling, the original speaker and statement day. Use the schema's tier: fact for official actions, published actual figures or completed transactions; party_statement for company guidance, management statements or officials' remarks; third_party_view for analyst, media or expert opinions; rumour for claims the item credits to unnamed sources or calls unconfirmed; an announcement or completed deal the item reports is a fact even when the reporter names no source.”
- Item header: “Officially or company-confirmed events are facts; unnamed-source claims and unconfirmed reports are rumours; quoted company or official statements are party statements; the journalist's own judgements are third-party views.”
- The passage itself: “The police said the fraud charges allege that the trio engaged in a conspiracy to commit fraud linked to the purchase of servers from three suppliers – Dell, Super Micro Computer and Asus.”

I treated the passage as the article’s retelling of what police said, but chose `third_party_view` because the item’s wording says journalist judgements are third-party views and does not present this passage as a direct police quote.

Other tiers I considered and rejected:

- `party_statement`: It was plausible because the claim is attributed to police, and the item header says quoted official statements are party statements. But the passage is indirect reporting (“The police said…”), not a quoted police statement.
- `fact`: The charges are an official action, but the statement about the alleged conspiracy is an allegation in the charges, not a finding of fact. The system reserves `fact` for official actions, published actual figures, or completed transactions.
- `rumour`: Rejected because the item attributes the allegation to police and charges; it is not an unnamed-source claim or an unconfirmed report.

On reflection, the passage’s explicit attribution to police makes `party_statement` the better tier under the supplied instructions, even though it is paraphrased rather than quoted directly.
