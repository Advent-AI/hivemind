2. **Contradictions.**

   - **Action versus original statement.** “took the action” and “without attribution” make the actor the original party even when the article does not identify that actor as the source. The standard requires “原始判断者”; T7 asks for “the original source and statement day,” and T8 asks for “original attribution.” [extraction.py:58](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/extraction.py:58); [standard §5.4](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:378); [categories T7:29](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/prompts/categories-v54.json:29); [T8:33](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/prompts/categories-v54.json:33).
   - **Action day versus statement day.** “The day that party made the statement **or took the action**” can select a known action date when the original statement date is unknown. That conflicts with “原始发言日不知道就写不知道” and T7’s “statement day.” The announcement example itself does not show this conflict because it names an announcement. [extraction.py:62](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/extraction.py:62); [standard §5.4](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:385); [categories T7:29](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/prompts/categories-v54.json:29).
   - **Publication-day exception.** “A party’s own release, filing, call or analysis is dated its publication day” can conflict with “empty when the item gives no day” and “never the publication day in place of an unknown day” in the preceding sentence. An own analysis can repeat an earlier statement whose original day is unknown. [extraction.py:63](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/extraction.py:63); [standard §5.4](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:385).
