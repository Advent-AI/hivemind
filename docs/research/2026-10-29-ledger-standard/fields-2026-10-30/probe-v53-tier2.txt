The request says: “quoted company or official statements are party statements.” It also says to “Identify the speaker and, for a retelling, the original speaker and statement day” and defines the tier as “party_statement for company guidance, management statements or officials' remarks.” E1 reports Nvidia’s announcement and states that “the company said Thursday,” so I treated it as Nvidia’s statement retold by CNBC.

I rejected **fact** because this is a company announcement about a program, not an official action, published actual figure, or completed transaction. I rejected **third_party_view** because the statement is attributed to Nvidia, not a journalist’s judgement or an analyst or expert opinion. I rejected **rumour** because the report does not attribute the program claim to unnamed sources or describe it as unconfirmed.
