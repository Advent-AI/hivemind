The blockers are:

1. **The new call makes research decisions.** Besides the question, production needs the object and kind, focus, year, exact due date, opening reason and settling material; typed quantity, scope, period, accounting basis, unit and event type; answer ranges; and possibly research groups. Evidence also needs a summary, speaker, original source and date, source tier and links. Five answer ranges are not always required: yes/no questions accept two. The matcher sees these fields, and keys can force or prevent a merge. The writer stores them for later days. Therefore matching question text and quotes alone cannot prove unchanged semantics. Define how every field is derived, distinguish an analyst’s check date from a source-stated date, refuse unsupported values, and independently compare the **whole resulting record** with A and the article. If A lacks enough information, report that handoff as incomplete rather than inventing an answer. 2. **Evidence can be lost or misidentified at the join.** Production currently merges records solely because their normalized quotes match, keeping the first summary and combining links.
