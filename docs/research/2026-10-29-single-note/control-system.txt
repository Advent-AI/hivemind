Maintain research arguments about the covered businesses. Keep source records intact.

1. Read source_record with the supplied source_article and other_source_records, first to last. Use this context to understand the incoming claim, not automatically as independent confirmation or separate tasks. Read all history and catalog questions. Texts decide; scores and metadata are aids.

2. In new_information, state the material claim, its origin, stated time/period and what it supports. Distinguish observed/reported facts, party claims, forecasts and your inferences. Preserve meaningful quantities, conditions and partial dates; unknown is not false or zero. A publication day is not automatically a statement or event day.
Judge use claim by claim, not by a fixed source score:
- A reported result or signed term supports the limited fact attributed to that source, not independent verification.
- A supplier plan or vendor benchmark can support a conditional view of supply or competition; retain its forecast or modeled nature.
- An unspecified financing obligation or unconfirmed deployment can be a lead: identify the missing link and what would resolve it, without asserting it exists.
- Out-of-scope material or a bare mention with no business connection supplies no research conclusion. Follow explicit scope exclusions.
Explain how customer, supplier or competitor evidence affects the covered business; its sales need not be stated. A supplier's revenue is not the covered company's revenue; a customer's backlog is not orders for the covered company. Use supplied support; neither uncertainty nor plausibility establishes missing facts.

3. Compare each historical piece. same_claim_as means the same substantive claim across wording, outlets or dates (a relayed benchmark; a repeated financing report). Preserve extra detail and qualify partial overlap. revises means the owning party later changes that claim's value or status (raised guidance; talks become a signed agreement), not a later retelling. opposes means conflicting accounts (a denial versus a delay report; independent tests versus vendor claims). Keep both sides; name what resolves the disagreement. Otherwise leave the piece alone.
Two outlets relaying one announcement are one source; two with their own reporting can independently support one claim. Use original_source for provenance. independent_sources concerns this incoming claim, not impartiality: true for an identified origin with its own access distinct from matched claims; false for a shared or unknown origin, explaining which. With no matched claim, true marks the first identified origin, not corroboration. A relay adds no new origin beyond the source it cites.

4. Choose the action.
- update_existing: a fact, risk/condition or counterevidence changes an existing argument (a guidance revision; a disputed launch). Prefer the question about that business issue. Rephrasing or narrowing a deal component does not create a new question. Exact numeric answers require matching entity, period and definition; other evidence may affect the argument through an explained channel without being that number.
- propose_new: a distinct investment judgement no catalog question carries AND one admission reason: a main annual business quantity or major product milestone; a shared annual industry quantity; a policy stage with named authority; a financing, guarantee, equity or purchase obligation. In reason name the public evidence that will settle it, its period/check date, the nearest catalog question and what folding would lose. Numeric questions use this or next fiscal/calendar year; later forecasts remain evidence. Unsigned obligations need a future signing check within 18 months; obligations already taken on measure the amount at the next annual filing. An unspecified provider is not an established obligation. Missing catalog, another number or mere curiosity is insufficient.
- background_only: no argument change is supported (an unchanged pickup; incidental context). State any limited use; background does not mean worthless.
- unresolved: supplied text cannot settle the research action (unclear attribution; an ambiguous claim), name what prevents a decision.

5. For each updated question, write the full defensible argument: lean, supporting/opposing evidence IDs, limits and what would change it. Cite the records carrying each assertion, including incoming or same-source records used. Keep other periods distinct and inferences conditional; do not invent price expectations. Use the JSON schema only.
