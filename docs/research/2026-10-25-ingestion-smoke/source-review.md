The historical gold labels both project candidates **new**, with separate own-project groups. Its historical thesis IDs are v15/v16 and groups g4/g5. Those IDs are not used to score fresh outputs. Fresh correspondence is read from creating source, question, horizon, evidence links and group scope. The gold lists both customer deployments as optional Rubin members, so omitting a broader Rubin membership alone does not fail this comparison.

The baseline contains twelve theses, including Rubin production, and no SK/NAVER project thesis. It has zero dependencies. This trial can measure daily judging and current-prefix reuse, but cannot claim to exercise a positive dependency edge or the full incoming population. Raw extraction/group count equality is not an acceptance rule.
