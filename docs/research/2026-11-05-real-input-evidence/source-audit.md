# Source audit for the earnings-context study

Status: in progress; no sample or gold is accepted for scoring and no model comparison has been sent. The two-article packet and reference are AI-authored drafts, not the formal gold. Source and date checks were made against the stored inputs and live source pages on 2026-11-05. A matching current page establishes what it says now; it does not establish that the complete page was unchanged since publication. It must not be described as an independently verified historical snapshot.

## Findings that change the setup

1. The existing frozen golden-v5 test contains 30 real stored articles. All 47 kept-item spans and all 267 non-kept spans are exact substrings of the articles currently stored in `poc/data/items-nvda-2026-07-01-10-27.sqlite`: 314/314 span checks; 30/30 input IDs found. This proves only text matching, not correct dates, investment interpretation, independence from earlier runs, or the suitability of their research contexts. This gold and the old scores remain frozen; it is not legitimate to claim its previously measured test articles are unseen.
2. `gnews:b6c2330e01a0c4ddb500583a` is tagged 2026-09-01 in the database but the stored article itself ends with `Update, September 3rd: Added more answers from Nvidia.` The historical 09-01 input is not established. Exclude that copy; don't silently strip the update and call the rest verified.
3. `gnews:5f2d05380f2023f53e22f149` is tagged 2026-10-03 in the database, while its [AOL source](https://www.aol.com/articles/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-just-045000000.html) shows 2026-10-04 04:50 UTC. Exclude pending source-history resolution.
4. `gnews:df63c0bccb7dbb77885ad5ef` has 2026-09-13 07:00 UTC in the database, while its [publisher page](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/09/13/here-are-all-the-companies-in-nvidias-99-billion/) shows Sep 13 at 1:23 PM EST. Same day but inconsistent time; do not rely on the stored timestamp for intra-day ordering. Independently verify the historical copy before inclusion.
5. The [SEC release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000073/q2fy27pr.htm) is dated August 26 and the database records the filing timestamp 2026-08-27T00:21:19Z, before both September 10 draft examples. The stored `text` drops financial tables: for example, non-GAAP operating income `63,956` is absent. The stored `text_page` includes that value and tables; it is the proposed report input, checked against the SEC source and not automatically treated as perfect. Release `text_page`: 22,347 characters. SEC EX-99.2 CFO commentary `text_page`: 19,722 characters. The reference packet currently uses EX-99.1 only; selecting release versus both documents is an experiment design choice, not a proven optimal context.
6. The two draft article URLs show 2026-09-10 on their source pages: [Wardogs](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/geforce-now-thursday-wardogs/) and [Skild](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/skild-ai-s1-physical-ai/). Their stored timestamps say 12:00Z, but the visible pages establish a date, not that exact time or historical immutability. Those articles are early examples only, not a diverse or accepted evaluation sample.

## Skild: near-contemporary archive found

The Internet Archive indexes a snapshot captured **2026-09-11 08:01:31 UTC**, the day after publication: <https://web.archive.org/web/20260911080131/https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/skild-ai-s1-physical-ai/>. A local compressed copy is `source-snapshots/skild-20260911.html.gz`; compressed SHA-256 `6800b25f18c47f1bc9f5ab73f08e02bd7e5b31bf28f02ef3b959bd4c4169c16e`, decompressed SHA-256 `9659d0ca90767bab7b934c66b35367b6356be5ff68954bd828d0d2b93dbc7df6`.

That snapshot's metadata reports publication **2026-09-10 16:30:35 UTC** and last modification **2026-09-10 17:21:38 UTC**. The database's `12:00:00Z` is earlier than both. Seventeen substantive article paragraphs in the snapshot match the stored article after whitespace normalization; a final link to further reading and website navigation are absent from the stored text. This is evidence that the stored content matches a near-contemporary page, not proof of the exact revision at publication or that no later change occurred before the archive capture. The current draft packet still holds the database timestamp; it must not be sent as a production-faithful, correctly timed input. A corrected, explicitly versioned experimental input would need to use a defensible availability time and record the provenance; do not overwrite the original database row.

## Near-contemporary capture census

The 12 candidate URLs were checked in the Internet Archive index for captures within three days of the stored date: 11 have at least one indexed capture, one (the Chanos Yahoo URL) has none in that window. Six indexed pages were downloaded and saved under `source-snapshots/` with HTML hashes in `draft-archive-index.json`; five remaining indexed captures could not be fetched in this pass because the connection was refused. An index entry alone is not a reviewed source.

For all six downloaded pages, source metadata gives a later same-day publication time than the database value: Wardogs 13:00:18Z vs 12:00Z; Skild 16:30:35Z vs 12:00Z; DLSS 13:00Z vs 07:00Z; $99bn portfolio 17:23Z vs 07:00Z; post-earnings portfolio 14:47Z vs 07:00Z; and the RTX Spark event 18:45:28Z vs 12:00Z. These are six checked source timestamps out of the 12 selected candidate records, not an estimate for the full corpus. The source metadata itself still needs checking for reliability, but this shows the stored clock times cannot be assumed exact.

The stored September 1 DLSS article also differs from the September 1 archive (for example, an on-page update later added September 3); the stored October 7 RTX article differs from its same-day archive in multiple paragraphs. The question of whether each difference changes an investment matter is not yet adjudicated. Both arms must see the same explicitly chosen historical version. Corrected experimental inputs must be new, named artifacts with source capture, content and timestamp provenance; they must not overwrite the original production records or be represented as what production actually received.

## Archive retrieval update and full-request reference review

After retrying the five previously refused snapshot downloads sequentially, 11 of the 12 candidates now have archived pages; the Chanos Yahoo URL still has no capture within the three-day search window. `audit_source_snapshots.py` reports that, of these 11 archived publisher timestamps, 10 are later than the database timestamp and one (the Europe science article) is earlier. This supersedes the earlier six-page intermediate census; none of these counts alone proves the original content version or generalizes to the corpus.

An independent AI reader reviewed the complete model-visible treatment requests and both references, with no model answers, in `complete-request-gold-review.md`. It found a material issue an article-only read had missed: the earlier earnings release already announced RTX Spark and DGX Station, so the October article's incremental material is preorder dates, availability and named partner activity, not an entirely new product announcement. It also identified potential confusion between the article's approximately $99 billion investment portfolio and the report's approximately $99 billion share-repurchase authorization. The complete request review confirms gold must distinguish source and timing. It did not resolve whether the Hugging Face acquisition is core or supporting, whether the Microsoft MXC announcement is sufficiently relevant to NVIDIA, or attribution for individual investment amounts. Readers disagree on these material matters; stop formal scoring until resolved against the signed standard with Alex. No gold is signed or frozen, and no model comparison has been sent.

The earlier proposal to replace the network transport with a custom one-attempt sender is withdrawn. An independent review showed this would require its own substantial failure, budget and identity design. The proposed comparison instead retains production's existing request transport and its at-most-three HTTP attempts, including the first, and will report incomplete or uncertain calls honestly. That change is a proposal and has not yet been independently reviewed, implemented for a live comparison, or sent.
