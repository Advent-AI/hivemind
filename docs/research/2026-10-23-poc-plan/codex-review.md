- **2. Paths for information from after the replay day**
  - **Confirmed — Historical provider edits.** Plan L256 captures today’s `"title + … + preview"` and L193 admits it by publication day. `02-classification:113`: “修正、更新、重述、编辑、类别改了，都是新版本，旧版本留着”; experience §十一.2 requires “只用当天以前的材料”. A receipt proves what was fetched today, not what that preview said historically. **No test covers provider edits or unavailable historical versions.**
  - **Confirmed — Feeder protection is narrower than claimed.** Plan L502 rejects only `"publication_day > day"`. This covers future-dated rows, as Task 12 tests demonstrate; it does not cover later-written content retaining an old publication date. Same governing sentences as above.
  - **Suspected — Future database state through search and matching.** Plan L413 searches `"the whole database open and closed"`; L382’s queries have no sequence cutoff. `00-system:56` permits only smaller-sequence records plus this round’s writes. Closed views are legitimate; later-created views, later closures and later replacements are not. A fresh, strictly sequential database avoids this; reused/resumed databases lack an explicit protection. **Tasks 8–9 have no future-state tests.**
  - **Suspected — Future conclusions through dependencies.** Plan L446 reads `"dependencies’ current conclusions"` without an as-of argument. D51 requires this round’s valid dependency conclusions; `00-system:56` limits visible records. Later successors, replacements or judgements can leak through the same reused-database path. **No corresponding test.**
  - **Confirmed — Pull’s date is explicit, its reads are not.** Plan L487 passes `"now_day"` but freezes whatever `"current_state"` returns. `08-pull-mode:17` requires replay time; sequence visibility still follows `00-system:56`. **Task 11 tests due dates, not future judgements.** L502 also iterates “each publication day”; quiet calendar days can therefore miss their scheduled freeze.
  - **Confirmed — Belief mapping has no historical cutoff.** Plan L517’s `beliefs.map(...)` accepts neither day nor sequence. Against experience §十一.2’s “当天以前”, it can map a historical belief using questions created later. **Task 13 tests matching shape, not historical availability.**

Experience §十一.2’s historical restriction also applies to those calls: June knowledge can affect questions supposedly formed in February. **No test or mitigation covers this.**
