**The plan is not ready to approve:** its measurement can miss lost questions and false merges, and several proposed rules go beyond the signed standard. For example, the stored week has a total-revenue question and a revenue-growth question that bypass the proposed same-type comparison; the plan’s own gold construction can miss that pair.

Findings are ranked by severity.

1. **The proposed gold can miss both false merges and missing required questions.** [Plan lines 100–101](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md:100) builds G1 from listed pairs plus unlisted pairs with the same object **and type**, and G2 from questions that already opened. In the v38 ledger, `v4` asks FY2027 total revenue and `v10` asks FY2027 revenue growth; match record 14 calls them different quantities. [D212](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/DECISIONS.md:2707) confirms that revenue/growth bypasses the same-measure gate. The pair can therefore be absent from G1 even if a future path merges it incorrectly. A required question never proposed or opened cannot enter G2 at all. The existing admission gold provides some missing-question coverage, but only for its specific cases ([D188](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/DECISIONS.md:2483)). A gold derived from source material, including required questions absent from the ledger and relevant cross-type pairs, is needed to detect these losses.

2. **The acceptance conditions allow material regressions to pass.** [Plan lines 124–126](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md:124) permit false merges up to the control’s count, even if the new arm loses a different required question. They count evidence as preserved if it is attached *anywhere*, even if it is attached to the wrong question. [D212](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/DECISIONS.md:2705) already records three material pieces under the wrong product question and says reducing page count did not restore dropped material. The evidence-dedup arm also has no stated acceptance threshold for G3 or a check that a different or richer claim remains visible to the judge; that was the loss behind [D204](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/DECISIONS.md:2611).

3. **H7’s proposed identity key can hide distinct evidence.** [Plan line 92](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md:92) treats equal speaker, date and numbers as one statement. One speaker can make two different claims about the same number on the same day, or add a qualification. Accurate fields do not establish equal meaning. The proposed single display also conflicts with the decision to show both source records to the judge, labelled as an opinion ([D201](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/DECISIONS.md:2587), [D204](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/DECISIONS.md:2612)). The plan names those decisions, but does not state this conflict.

4. **The claimed cause of the ACP split is unproven.** [Plan lines 12–16 and 68](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md:12) attribute the split to the “guarantee beside a financing” sentence. The sentence **is** in both [the collision prompt](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/prompts/collision-v1.txt:1) and [the signed standard](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:318); the plan is correct on that factual point. But v38 match records 6 and 10 record only the verdicts, not the instruction that caused them. The plan itself acknowledges that the model account has not been obtained ([lines 75–76](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md:75)). The two standard lines create a real boundary to resolve, but the records do not establish that this sentence caused the split or that every guarantee and financing within a plan must be separate.

5. **H1, H3 and the general rule add boundaries that the standard does not settle.** [Plan lines 80–82 and 86–88](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md:80) add “when uncertain, merge,” “one plan, one question,” and “one product generation, one question.” The signed standard does say one outcome can settle two questions ([line 316](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:316)), so that part is correctly attributed. It also requires separation where answers can move apart ([line 274](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:274)), and [D236](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/DECISIONS.md:2871) permits a launch **or** shipment question; it does not decide that all milestones are one question. D243’s caution about noisy extraction is not a decision to merge uncertain questions ([standard lines 153–156](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:153)). The v38 ledger’s `v11` launch and `v17` shipment are a concrete case requiring that distinction.

6. **H4 treats different periods as a settled duplicate.** [Plan line 89](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md:89) says FY2027 CPU revenue and calendar-2026 server CPU revenue are the same question under the standard. They differ in both scope and period. The standard’s duplicate test concerns another presentation of the **same result** ([line 289](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:289)); it separately treats the next period as succession ([line 317](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:317)). The earlier [Claude-labelled review](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-admission-v12-2026-10-26/docs/research/2026-10-28-quality-review/gold-admission.md:30) labels this pair duplicate, but is not an Alex-approved decision.

7. **H2 and H5 do not fully cover the existing identity contract.** H2’s proposed merge keeps the earlier question’s fixed outcomes and due date; the plan does not show how a later deadline remains answerable after the earlier deadline passes. The existing question and outcomes are immutable ([PRD 06 lines 8 and 14](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/06-views.md:8)); succession is continued tracking after an event question closes ([line 176](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/06-views.md:176)). H5 says code would retain only object, type and due year ([plan line 90](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md:90)); that omits scope, event type and other distinctions currently used by [identity.py](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/src/hivemind/views/identity.py:18). The earlier review says four twins were outside the top ten, while also saying collision judging ran on all 11 candidates ([report lines 40–46](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-admission-v12-2026-10-26/docs/research/2026-10-28-quality-review/report.md:40)); those statements do not establish that the judge saw each missing twin.
