**A serious finding remains:** the plan says every new regression stops an arm, but also says a newly opened trivial question will only be counted. For example, if the control keeps a minor candidate as evidence and the changed matcher opens it as a separate question, the plan gives two different acceptance rules.

1. **Serious — a new trivial question can pass.** [Plan lines 155–156]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md:155) make every candidate that was correct in the control and wrong in the new arm a stopping condition. But [line 165]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md:165) exempts a distinct but unworthy question from stopping a matching arm. The [signed standard]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html:281) requires both distinctness and worth. The plan needs to distinguish an **existing** unworthy opening, which can be reported as a remaining defect, from a **new** one caused by the changed arm, which its own regression rule says must stop it.

2. **Factual — the placement diagram overstates the one-home rule.** [Plan lines 33–34]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md:33) say placement files each statement under the narrowest existing question **or** this article’s candidate. The [underlying trace]( /Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/research/2026-10-28-retention-audit/root-trace-2026-10-28.md:7) also cautions that these are records, not 94 different facts. Losses common to both arms remain outside this review’s measurement scope.
