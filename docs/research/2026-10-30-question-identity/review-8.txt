**One serious finding remains:** the plan can accept a new duplicate if other duplicates are removed in the same arm.

1. **Serious — duplicate regressions can cancel out in the total.** [Plan lines 152–160](docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md#L152) compare each candidate with its expected outcome, but make missed merges an exception: the arm passes if their **number** is lower than the control. For example, two old duplicates could be fixed while a candidate that previously merged correctly opens a new duplicate; the total improves and the regression passes. This leaves part of the round-4 class open: the per-candidate record exposes the error, but the acceptance rule does not stop on it. The v38 census provides real merge cases to protect: [census lines 29–37](docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/census/admission-census.txt#L29) record 12 merges and three collision decisions classified as distinct within one matter.

2. **Factual, with measurement implications — the pipeline diagram puts placement after question matching.** [Plan lines 27–44](docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md#L27) show matching first and placement afterwards. In the current path, [round.py lines 103–130](src/hivemind/views/round.py#L103) obtains the extraction handover before matching, and [extraction.py lines 608–630](src/hivemind/extraction.py#L608) shows that placement is part of that handover. Placement can also remove a candidate when none of its evidence remains. The later measurement section correctly says placement may need a fresh answer when the open questions change, but the diagram and [line 145](docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md#L145) overstate that score changes come only from matching: placement sampling can change the candidate set too.

3. **Factual — the candidate-list description is too narrow.** [Plan line 34](docs/research/2026-10-30-question-identity/plan.md#L34) says the first route finds questions with the *same identity key*. [matching.py lines 122–145](src/hivemind/views/matching.py#L122) also includes compatible keys with overlapping periods; [store.py lines 161–172](src/hivemind/views/store.py#L161) specifies the overlap and wildcard rules. This matters to H5 because changing a period start can affect which questions are compared even when their full keys are unequal.
