**The text can drop evidence the standard keeps**, especially product comparisons, meaningful new details described as background, and optional post-earnings price reactions. For example, §5.3 explicitly says a competitor’s product comparison cannot be removed under the product-detail rule, but the text gives no such boundary.

I audited the text as written, using [version 4 of the standard](/Users/lchingen/hivemind-extraction-quality-2026-10-28/docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html) as the only ruler. “Optional” means the standard permits keeping or omitting an item; a command to drop it is therefore marked **WIDER-DROP**. Where an example lacks enough context to determine its importance, I say so.

## Clause-by-clause audit

### Instruction and first drop rule

1. **“Before you return the answer, check it once: read each evidence item back as the analyst following the company, and drop it when it is one of these.”**  
   Standard, §5.1: **“一个在追这家公司的分析员，读到会在本周笔记里写下来，因为它让某个问题的答案更可能或更不可能。”** §5.2: **“下面的不留清单不能用来删掉分析员会记的陈述”**.  
   **CONTRADICTS.** The instruction makes membership in the following list a reason to drop, without stating the standard’s rule that the list cannot remove something the analyst would record.

2. **“A matter that moves none of the analyst's questions about the company or its customers, suppliers, competitors, regulators or the economy”**  
   Standard, §5.1: **“问题不限于库里已有的，也不限于本公司：客户、供应商、竞争者、监管、宏观都算”**.  
   **CONSISTENT.** It uses the same broad scope and excludes matters that change none of the analyst’s answers.

3. **“another party's own news that touches none of the company's markets”**  
   Standard, §5.3: **“和公司所有市场都无关的别家公司的估值、股价、交易”** and **“别家公司的动作和经营数字不能用这一行删掉，它们留不留按 5.1 的规则判。”**  
   **WIDER-DROP.** It expands the D238 rejection from valuation, share price and deals to all another party’s news, including actions and operating figures the standard expressly sends back to §5.1.

4. **“a customer opened a new head office”**  
   Standard, §5.1: **“客户、供应商、竞争者、伙伴的有规模、有日期或有名字的动作，以及它们公布的数字”**; §5.3: **“别家公司的动作和经营数字不能用这一行删掉”**.  
   **EXAMPLE-DISAGREES.** A customer’s named action must be judged by its relevance under §5.1; the example supplies no context establishing that it is irrelevant.

5. **“a supplier's unrelated unit won an award”**  
   Standard, §5.1: **“一个在追这家公司的分析员，读到会在本周笔记里写下来，因为它让某个问题的答案更可能或更不可能。”**  
   **CONSISTENT.** As described, an award won by an unrelated unit is an example of a matter that does not move an answer.

6. **“routine administration”**  
   Standard, §5.1: **“留不留由上面的规则判”**; §5.3: **“不在清单上的句子，用 5.1 的规则逐句判”**.  
   **NO-SOURCE.** The standard has no blanket rejection for administration; its relevance depends on the statement.

7. **“a regional head's pay package”**  
   Standard, §5.1: **“留不留由上面的规则判”**.  
   **NO-SOURCE.** The standard does not list pay packages as a rejected class, and this example does not provide enough context to decide its relevance.

8. **“the rent on one office”**  
   Standard, §6: **“对公司规模来说是小事的”** can be **“辅助”**; §5.1 says **“留不留由上面的规则判”**.  
   **EXAMPLE-DISAGREES.** The text makes one office’s rent a categorical drop even though a small matter can be supporting evidence when it passes the analyst test.

9. **“a shareholder proposal on reporting”**  
   Standard, §5.1: **“一件新发生的事、一个新数字、一个新的具体判断，是增量，不能漏”**, subject to the analyst test.  
   **NO-SOURCE.** The standard does not reject shareholder proposals as a class, and the example gives too little context to determine whether this one matters.

### Background and typical claims

10. **“Background the analyst already knew: a partnership, programme or policy already in place, described again”**  
    Standard, §5.3: **“重提的过去实际数可选；重提的季度指引和仍然有效的预测、计划按本来分量；看不出是旧事的不算重提”**.  
    **WIDER-DROP.** Repetition or an existing arrangement alone does not establish that the whole statement is rejectable; a still-effective plan retains its original weight.

11. **“a position held for years”**  
    Standard, §5.1 gives the keep example **“CFO 说十年前建的工厂仍满负荷”** and says **“不是常识的，是新信息……照上面的规则判”**.  
    **WIDER-DROP.** A long-held state or position can contain a newly reported, analyst-relevant fact; its duration alone is not a rejection rule.

12. **“the company's history or headcount”**  
    Standard, §5.3 rejects **“记者的背景和框架”**, with the boundary **“没有数字和具体叙事的主张和背景不收；怎么分见 5.1”**.  
    **WIDER-DROP.** History and headcount can contain a new, relevant fact or number, so their subject alone does not justify dropping them.

13. **“the backstory of a case”**  
    Standard, §5.3: **“新加坡转运案件的概况和被告个人资产”** are **“可选，收不收都不扣分”**; for background generally, **“怎么分见 5.1”**.  
    **WIDER-DROP.** A categorical drop can remove optional case background or a relevant new detail.

14. **“A typical claim without a figure and a specific account”**  
    Standard, §5.1: **“这类陈述没有数字和具体的叙事就不收”**, where the class is **“常识，就是跟这家公司的分析员读这篇之前就已经知道的”**.  
    **CONSISTENT.** Read as a rule about typical claims that are already common knowledge, it follows the standard’s test.

15. **“that a firm leads, competes or can take share”**  
    Standard, §5.1: **“谁领先、谁在竞争”** are examples of common knowledge; its rejected examples include **“博通 CEO 说能从英伟达抢份额”**.  
    **CONSISTENT.** These examples match the standard’s typical claims, provided they are common knowledge rather than a new specific judgment.

16. **“that customers want cheaper options”**  
    Standard, §5.1: **“不是常识的，是新信息……照上面的规则判，分析员会记就留。”**  
    **NO-SOURCE.** The standard does not give this example, and the wording alone does not establish whether it is common knowledge or a new specific demand observation.

17. **“that demand is strong or supply tight”**  
    Standard, §5.1 lists **“需求强、供给紧”** as common-knowledge examples and **“Blackwell 和 Rubin 仍然供不应求”** as not kept.  
    **CONSISTENT.** These are the standard’s own types of typical claim when they restate known conditions.

18. **“A figure alone does not keep it”**  
    Standard, §5.1: **“有数字也要过上面的规则”**.  
    **CONSISTENT.** A number does not replace the analyst’s relevance judgment.

19. **“the leading carmaker has held a third of its home market for a decade”**  
    Standard, §5.1: **“作者说‘英特尔多年领先，占 CPU 市场 59%’，和英伟达的问题关系不大，分析员不会记。”**  
    **NO-SOURCE.** The standard’s example includes an explicit lack of relevance; this example gives no covered company or context, so the number and duration alone do not establish a drop.

### Product details

20. **“A detail with no business scale”**  
    Standard, §5.3: **“没有业务规模的产品、游戏、驱动、跑分细节”**; its boundary says **“对手拿它和本公司产品比的说法不能用这一行删掉，按 5.1 判”**.  
    **WIDER-DROP.** It omits the competitor-comparison boundary and can remove a comparison the standard treats as supporting.

21. **“a product's specifications, benchmark, feature, compatibility, content or one model's price”**  
    Standard, §5.3: **“对手拿它和本公司产品比的说法不能用这一行删掉，按 5.1 判”**; glossary, 七类驱动: **“竞争与份额：对手的产品、价格、开店、赢单。”**  
    **WIDER-DROP.** A competitor’s benchmark or price can be relevant evidence even when expressed as a single product detail.

22. **“the release of a free tool or model”**  
    Standard, §5.1: **“公司自己的数字、指引或决定”**; §5.2: **“有名有姓的动作缺金额、缺日期也是证据”**; §5.3 says **“伙伴、客户采用或集成的动作不是产品细节”**.  
    **WIDER-DROP.** Free release alone does not decide whether a named launch, adoption or integration is analyst-relevant.

23. **“a change to what a product costs is kept”**  
    Standard, §5.1 includes **“公司取消软件订阅费，生产使用免费”**; §6 lists **“公司取消软件订阅费”** as supporting.  
    **CONSISTENT.** The exception protects the kind of pricing change the standard explicitly keeps.

24. **“a game, app or service-region detail”**  
    Standard, §5.3 rejects **“没有业务规模的产品、游戏、驱动、跑分细节”** but says competitor comparisons and customer or partner adoption cannot be dropped under that row.  
    **WIDER-DROP.** The examples lack those boundaries and can remove a relevant comparison, adoption or expansion.

### Views, prices and repeated facts

25. **“A general view or a quote that only characterises”**  
    Standard, §5.3: **“作者的一般性风险清单和没有推导的看法”** and **“只有形容的引语”** are not kept.  
    **CONSISTENT.** The rejection is supported when the view is general and lacks derivation, or the quotation only characterises.

26. **“a rating tally”**  
    Standard, §5.1: **“不是常识的，是新信息，比如一份新报告的判断、一个新的市场数据……照上面的规则判”**.  
    **NO-SOURCE.** The standard does not categorically reject rating tallies, and this example lacks enough context to decide whether one is useful evidence.

27. **“the stock looks cheap”**  
    Standard, §5.3 rejects **“作者的一般性风险清单和没有推导的看法”** and keeps **“有假设、有推导、有时点的条件估值”**.  
    **CONSISTENT.** As written, the example gives an unsupported valuation opinion with no derivation.

28. **“a risk list without derivation”**  
    Standard, §5.3 rejects **“作者的一般性风险清单和没有推导的看法”**.  
    **CONSISTENT.** It matches the rejected general risk-list class.

29. **“a new era has arrived”**  
    Standard, §5.3 rejects **“只有形容的引语”**, with **“a pivotal moment”** as an example.  
    **CONSISTENT.** The quotation only characterises and supplies no substantive claim.

30. **“A share-price move or an investor's position”**  
    Standard, §5.3: **“股价涨跌、投资人仓位、技术面”** are generally not kept, but **“‘财报后股价反应’可选”**.  
    **WIDER-DROP.** The blanket share-price rule removes a post-earnings reaction that the standard permits keeping.

31. **“the news behind a move is judged on its own”**  
    Standard, §5.3: **“股价反应背后的那条新闻不能用这一行删掉，按 5.1 判”**.  
    **CONSISTENT.** It preserves the standard’s boundary for the underlying news.

32. **“a second item for the same fact (keep the fuller one)”**  
    Standard, §5.3: **“同一事实的复述”**, illustrated by **“一篇稿子用五句话说一个部署是一条”** and **“标题和正文说同一件事”**.  
    **CONSISTENT.** Within this request’s evidence list, it follows the rule against repeating the same fact; the instruction to choose the fuller item is not stated in the standard, but does not itself require dropping a distinct fact.

### Keep exceptions and final instruction

33. **“Keep, even when it looks like the above: a forecast, a rumour, a counter-example, a distant or conditional statement”**  
    Standard, §5.2: **“预测、传闻、反证、远期、条件情景都是证据”** and **“不因为‘还没发生’‘不是官方’‘离现在远’‘和我们方向相反’就不留”**; §5.1: **“留不留由上面的规则判”**.  
    **NARROWER-DROP.** The standard protects these characteristics from being reasons for rejection, but still applies the analyst test; the text says to keep every such item.

34. **“a named party's action without an amount or a date”**  
    Standard, §5.2: **“有名有姓的动作缺金额、缺日期也是证据，分量可以低一点”**; §5.1: **“留不留由上面的规则判”**.  
    **NARROWER-DROP.** Missing amount or date cannot itself cause a drop, but a named action still has to pass the analyst test.

35. **“guidance, a forecast or a plan still in force, even when retold”**  
    Standard, §5.3: **“重提的季度指引和仍然有效的预测、计划按本来分量”**.  
    **CONSISTENT.** The exception protects the retold guidance, forecasts and plans the standard says retain their original weight.

36. **“a customer's, supplier's or competitor's specific action or figure”**  
    Standard, §5.1: **“客户、供应商、竞争者、伙伴的有规模、有日期或有名字的动作，以及它们公布的数字”**, and **“四类描述的是会记的陈述长什么样，不是‘属于四类就一定留’”**.  
    **NARROWER-DROP.** This says to keep every such action or figure, whereas the standard first applies the analyst test.

37. **“a rival bank now offers the same fee waiver”**  
    Standard, glossary: **“竞争与份额：对手的产品、价格、开店、赢单”**; §5.1 keeps relevant competitor actions under the analyst test.  
    **CONSISTENT.** A rival’s fee offer is an example of competitor pricing that can change an analyst’s answer.

38. **“a partner's or customer's adoption or integration”**  
    Standard, §5.3: **“伙伴、客户采用或集成的动作不是产品细节（5.1 第 2 类）”**; §5.1: **“不是‘属于四类就一定留’”**.  
    **NARROWER-DROP.** The standard protects adoption and integration from the product-detail rejection, but still judges each under §5.1.

39. **“the launch, shipment, delay or cancellation of a product that sells or will sell at scale”**  
    Standard, glossary: **“产品周期：发布、出货、试验、获批、延期”**; §6 includes **“关键产品进度”** in key judgments.  
    **CONSISTENT.** These are product-cycle events that can carry the business-scale information the standard asks the analyst to assess.

40. **“a change to the plan or configuration of the product that carries the company's value”**  
    Standard, §6: **“它承载价值的那一代产品及其配置、过渡方案”**.  
    **CONSISTENT.** The exception covers a product configuration or transition the standard identifies as potentially central.

41. **“a new policy or regulatory step”**  
    Standard, §5.1: **“政策或监管的决定或步骤”**; §5.3: **“官员宣布的政策步骤不能用这一行删掉，按 5.1 判”**.  
    **NARROWER-DROP.** The standard requires a §5.1 judgment rather than automatically keeping every new policy step.

42. **“a valuation with its assumptions and date”**  
    Standard, §5.3: **“有假设、有推导、有时点的条件估值要留”**; §6: **“给出可检验的估值假设”** is supporting.  
    **NARROWER-DROP.** The text omits the standard’s derivation requirement and can keep an unsupported valuation merely because it has assumptions and a date.

43. **“Return only the items you keep.”**  
    Standard, §5.1: **“从第一段读到最后一段，分析员会记的都留。一页有八条就是八条，不挑”**.  
    **CONSISTENT.** As an output instruction after the review, it does not itself change the keep decision; it does not, however, recover evidence absent from the list being reviewed.

## Keep rules missing from the exceptions

These omissions matter because the earlier drop list can catch the same material.

- **Other companies’ operating figures.** §5.3 says **“别家公司的动作和经营数字不能用这一行删掉，它们留不留按 5.1 的规则判”**; §6 names **“别家公司自己的经营数字（收入、利润率、积压订单）”** as supporting. The keep list names customer, supplier and competitor figures, but omits a partner’s figures and other relevant companies’ figures; **“another party's own news”** can sweep them out.

- **Competitor comparisons of product details.** §5.3 says **“对手拿它和本公司产品比的说法不能用这一行删掉，按 5.1 判”**; §6 gives **“对手自己模拟的 2.4 倍性能比较”** as supporting. The product-detail drop has no corresponding comparison exception.

- **Optional items.** §5.3 and §6 allow **“财报后股价反应”**, **“重提的过去实际数”**, and the specified case background as **“可选”**. The text categorically drops price moves and broadly drops old background, without saying these items may be kept.

- **New specific information that resembles a familiar claim.** §5.1 says **“不是常识的，是新信息，比如一份新报告的判断、一个新的市场数据、刚发生的事的细节……分析员会记就留。”** The exceptions do not protect a new specific judgment, market observation or event detail when it resembles the listed typical claims.

- **Relevant named claims and estimates.** §5.1 includes **“具名一方的主张或估计”**, including **“CFO 说十年前建的工厂仍满负荷”**. The exceptions protect forecasts and actions but do not generally protect a named party’s relevant observation about a longstanding condition from the background rule.

- **The general boundary over all reject rules.** §5.2 says **“下面的不留清单不能用来删掉分析员会记的陈述”**. An exception list cannot fully replace this rule: the standard requires the analyst test for every applicable boundary, including cases the exception list does not name.

## Internal contradictions

- **Other-party news versus specific actions and figures.** **“another party's own news that touches none of the company's markets”** is dropped, while **“a customer's, supplier's or competitor's specific action or figure”** is kept. A specific action or figure by such a party can satisfy both descriptions, and the text does not say which instruction wins.

- **Existing arrangements versus effective plans.** **“a partnership, programme or policy already in place, described again”** is dropped, while **“a plan still in force, even when retold”** is kept. A continuing programme or partnership can also be a plan still in force.

- **Product details versus competitor actions.** **“a product's specifications, benchmark, feature, compatibility, content or one model's price”** is dropped, while **“a customer's, supplier's or competitor's specific action or figure”** is kept. A competitor’s new model price or benchmark can satisfy both.

- **Free releases versus scaled launches and adoption.** **“the release of a free tool or model”** is dropped, while a scaled product **“launch”** and **“a partner's or customer's adoption or integration”** are kept. A free product can be launched at scale or integrated by a partner.

- **General or typical views versus forecasts and conditional statements.** A **“general view”** and **“a typical claim without a figure and a specific account”** are dropped, while **“a forecast”** or **“a distant or conditional statement”** is kept. A vague forecast or conditional view can meet both descriptions.

## Three findings most likely to remove real evidence

1. **The product-detail rule omits the competitor-comparison boundary.** It can drop a benchmark or price comparison that §5.3 expressly protects and §6 can count as supporting.

2. **The background rule treats age, familiarity and subject as reasons to drop.** It can remove a newly reported fact about a longstanding condition, a meaningful case detail, or an optional restated actual.

3. **The D238 rule expands “valuation, share price or deals” into all another party’s news.** It can sweep away relevant actions or operating figures that §5.3 explicitly requires the analyst to judge under §5.1.
