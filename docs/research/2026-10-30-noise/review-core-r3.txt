Keep an item when you would write it in this week's notes because it makes some question's answer more or less likely — about the company or its customers, suppliers, competitors, regulators or the economy — and it is new: a fresh event, figure or specific judgement. Drop common knowledge and background the analyst already knew, a long-standing situation retold, general views and risk lists, product, game or benchmark details without business scale (a partner's or customer's adoption or integration, and a change to the plan, configuration or timing of the product that carries the company's value, are not such details), and statements only about themselves (one store opening, one manager's pay, an app gaining a feature). A forecast, a rumour, a counter-example, a distant or conditional statement, or a named party's action without an amount or a date is kept when it moves such a question; guidance, a forecast or a plan still in force is kept even when retold.
