The sentence that led me to keep e2 was: “Their reactor, the Ward250, went critical on June 18, and it is now generating 100 kilowatts of electricity.”

The relevant evidence rule is: “(2) A customer's, supplier's, competitor's or partner's action or reported figure with a size, date or name that bears on the company's demand, supply, input costs, competition, prices or products.” The 100-kilowatt output is a dated, quantified figure about a partner’s reactor and bears on the proposed data-center partnership.

Two rejected statements:

- “The Ward250’s helium cooling system, combined with Nvidia’s new technology, will allow the data center to operate with almost no water use.” Rejected under: “a product feature, performance, compatibility, retail price or content detail that gives no business scale (units, revenue, customers or capacity)”.
- “Nvidia recently announced its new AI factory design, DSX, which uses a highly water-efficient direct liquid cooling system.” Rejected under the same product-detail line: it describes a product feature without business scale.
