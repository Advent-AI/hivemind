Read-only review. Scope: one prompt block, src/hivemind/prompts/review-v64.txt, the check that closes the extraction request. It
replaces src/hivemind/prompts/review-v63.txt. Out of scope: question opening, record fields, matching, code, the role text's own
wording (src/hivemind/prompts/role-v64.txt, identical to role-v53, is context: the check is read after it), and the gold.

The ruler: the signed extraction standard docs/prd/05-extraction-standard.html (version 4), sections 5.1, 5.2, 5.3 (the not-kept
table and its boundary column) and 6; decisions D240, D243, D238 in docs/DECISIONS.md; AGENTS.md §6 (a prompt carries the standard,
not new rules; a missing amount, date or scale is never on its own a reason to drop).

What changed and why: in review-v63 a closing list ("Keep these whenever they move such a question; none is dropped for the reasons
above: ...") overrode every "usually fail" line; asked afterwards, the model named that list or one of its kinds as what kept 26 of 31
statements both the gold and a blind reader reject (product details, an officer's pay, an office's rent, a shareholder vote, a game
supporting a graphics feature). v64 attaches each exception to the line it limits, as the standard's boundary column does ("不能用这一
行删掉，按 5.1 判"), cuts the closing list, and says once that fitting one of the four kinds does not keep an item (5.1) and that a
missing amount, date or scale never drops one (D240).

Check, sentence by sentence and line by line:
1. Every sentence about what to keep or drop traces to a line of the standard or a decision: cite it. List any sentence with no source.
2. Contradictions with the standard: a line that could drop something the standard keeps (especially core under section 6, guidance,
   in-force forecasts and plans, a policy step, a competitor's comparison, a partner's adoption, another company's actions and
   operating figures, a conditional valuation, the news behind a share-price move), or keep something the standard rejects.
3. Exceptions that v63's closing list carried and v64 lost, or that v64 now scopes wrongly (to the wrong line, or globally).
4. Whether "What a line names after "not" is not dropped by that line; the check judges it" can be misread (v62 used "These are not
   by themselves reasons to drop; judge them by the check" and the model read it as a list of reasons to drop partner adoptions).
5. Contradictions with the role text the check is read after.
Rank findings most serious first, quote the words, say what the standard says, and propose the smallest wording fix. Say plainly when
a line is fine. No new rules.
