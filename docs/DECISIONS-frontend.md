# Frontend decisions in force

The presentation decisions of the Thesis Library and Explore Ideas workspaces that stand today, one line each, in number order. The [UI specification](poc/thesis-ui.md) and the code under `poc/ui/src/` hold them in full; the decisions superseded or retired are in the [index](DECISIONS.md#index), and every record is in the [log](DECISIONS-log.md). A new presentation decision is recorded like any other (AGENTS §11) and its line added or changed here.

- **D65** (amended by D313, D314): Focus outlines are disabled throughout the page's own stylesheet, sliders and buttons included.

- **D72** (amended by D124): (see the log)

- **D74**: Canvas cards show no change-summary line ('1 change | since…' or 'no change since…'); the chart and detail panel keep the change history.
